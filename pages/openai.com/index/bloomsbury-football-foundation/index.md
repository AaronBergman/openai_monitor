# Using ChatGPT to accelerate a charity’s growth

Meet Charlie who is transforming how Bloomsbury Football identifies funders and expands operations across the UK.

![Children run a football drill as Charlie watches.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6d7JeSCtWuEkkbttis4NRP/9918b89291655137f2a62f4799e4e6c9/bloomsbury-football-hero.jpg?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

> “There are a lot of things stopping young people playing. We exist to try and break down those barriers.”

—Charlie, CEO, Bloomsbury Football Foundation

## Helping support Britain’s future

When Charlie started Bloomsbury Football Foundation, it was just him with a bag of footballs and four kids on a pitch in Camden. Today, the charity supports 7000 children from disadvantaged backgrounds across London.

As they grow, ChatGPT has become a pivotal tool in helping them manage daily admin, support coaching sessions, identify potential new funders and plan future expansions. Crucially, it helps the charity’s Founder & CEO, Charlie, and his team spend less time at their desks and more on the pitch with the kids.
