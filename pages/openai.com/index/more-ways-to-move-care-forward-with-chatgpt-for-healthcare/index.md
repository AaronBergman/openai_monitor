Healthcare teams need to piece together information from the EHR, hospital policies, scheduling systems, and payer portals to deliver care and manage operations. With ChatGPT for Healthcare, we’re bringing the tools teams already use and the medical evidence they need into one platform to help them spend less time navigating systems and more time caring for patients.

Today, we’re sharing [_advances in computer-use agents_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-for-healthcare-teams/>), which lets AI agents carry out multi-step work across existing healthcare software. We’re also improving trusted clinical search, which finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence, and introducing more than 20 new plugins to the healthcare plugin ecosystem. 

Together, these capabilities help healthcare teams find the information they need, make decisions grounded in medical evidence, and carry out next steps across the systems they already use. 

## Carry work forward across healthcare systems with computer-use agents

Organizations often need to move information between disconnected systems to prepare documentation, coordinate care, and complete administrative tasks. Computer-use agents can help with these steps by navigating the software that teams already use: reading screens, clicking, typing, and moving between applications.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer-use agents for healthcare, including more capable, safe, and affordable models for clinical and administrative workflows, and access controls and audit logs for secure deployments. We’re also sharing our work with healthcare organizations that are deploying agents, including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health, to process referrals, move documents between systems, and bring together the information care teams need. In the demo below, a clinician uses Voice in the ChatGPT app to ask for help following up on an MRI request. The agent reviews the chart and drafts an order and patient reply for review. 

Healthcare organizations can start with a workflow that spans existing applications, set which applications agents can access, and establish clear controls for which steps require human review. To support HIPAA-compliant workflows involving protected health information, organizations must use eligible services covered by their BAA with OpenAI, apply required security and retention settings, and extend protections to connected systems. Content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train models.

Read more about our computer use research, customer stories, and demonstrations in [_Advancing computer-use agents for healthcare teams_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-for-healthcare-teams/>).

## Stronger clinical answers, everywhere teams work

We’re improving trusted clinical search in ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, including how ChatGPT finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence. These updates help clinicians connect relevant evidence to clinical questions and translate it into practical next steps, with citations they can inspect.

To measure the impact of these updates, in a blind evaluation, physicians reviewed responses to real-world clinical questions across multiple specialties, including multi-turn conversations. In over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated experience more highly across all eight dimensions evaluated, including factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness.
