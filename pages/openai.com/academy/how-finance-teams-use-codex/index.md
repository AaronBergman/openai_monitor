# How finance teams use ChatGPT Work

See how finance teams can use ChatGPT Work to build review-ready assets for monthly business reviews, reporting, variance analysis, and planning.

With ChatGPT Work, finance teams can turn real work inputs into review-ready deliverables faster. Start from close workbooks, revenue and expense dashboards, forecast updates, prior monthly business reviews, and owner notes you already use. ChatGPT Work helps turn that context into concrete assets your team can review, refine, and share, without needing to code. Use it to spend less time assembling the first draft and more time shaping the story, validating the numbers, and preparing for the next decision.

Learn more about using ChatGPT for everyday finance work in our [on-demand webinar⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://academy.openai.com/home/clubs/work-users-ynjqu/videos/codex-for-everyday-work-recording-2026-05-06>).
