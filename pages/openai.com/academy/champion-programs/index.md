# OpenAI Champion Programs

For the people influencing AI adoption across their organization.

Successful AI adoption depends on more than access to tools, it requires people across the organization working together to turn new AI capabilities into lasting business value. Those people are OpenAI Champions.  
  
OpenAI Champion Programs build the workforce capabilities organizations need to lead, deploy, enable, and integrate AI across the business.

### The Champion journey

Whether you’re shaping AI strategy, driving change or building AI workflows for your team, we’re here to connect you with the people and context you need to move adoption forward confidently.

#### Learn the foundations

Build a stronger understanding of what it takes to support AI adoption and the role you can play.

#### Apply it to your work

Use guides, examples, and reusable resources to support your team’s workflows, and day-to-day work.

#### Connect with peers

Learn from other champions working through similar questions and challenges.

#### Scale what works

Extend proven approaches across teams, build support around what’s working, and help adoption grow over time.

### Explore OpenAI Champion Programs

Champion Programs bring together learning and engagement through the Champion Community on OpenAI Academy, along with a more dedicated space for deeper engagement in the OpenAI Enterprise Champion Network.

![Abstract gradient background blending soft teal, peach, and beige tones with blurred orange shapes.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/7aewdKtyQgljFWykma0GcG/b487942ad1558cac60ae0b67ced39175/1.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Champion Community on OpenAI Academy

The latest guidance, examples, and insights from OpenAI, and opportunities to engage with a broad community of champions.  
  
Explore educational content, attend webinars, access reusable resources and participate in monthly challenges to learn what’s working, get inspired, and bring useful ideas back to your organization.

**Helpful links:**

  * [**The AI Champion Role** ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://academy.openai.com/home/clubs/champions-ecqup/resources/the-ai-champion-role>)**:** Read to learn more about how Champions influence adoption.

  * [**Upcoming Champion Community Events** ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://academy.openai.com/home/clubs/champions-ecqup/events>)**:** RSVP to learn more from other Champions. We’re always adding new events so be sure to check this page regularly.

#### OpenAI Enterprise Champion Network

 _Open to customers on OpenAI Enterprise plan._

A dedicated space for deeper engagement with fellow champions and OpenAI.  
  
Join smaller, discussion-based events shaped around role, industry, company size, and stage of adoption. Learn from real champion examples to see how other enterprise customers are scaling AI adoption, and take part in product discussions and feedback opportunities, to help shape the future of OpenAI.
