# ChatGPT Codex settings

Make ChatGPT Codex work the way you want, with fewer interruptions.

[Download Codex(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/codex/for-work/>)

Loading…

Share

You can access settings from the menu in the bottom left corner of ChatGPT Codex. For your first few tasks, focus on a few key settings: personalization, prevent sleep, detail level, and appearance.

**General > Prevent sleep while running** keeps your computer awake while ChatGPT Codex is running. This is useful for longer tasks. If your computer goes to sleep, ChatGPT Codex may stop working.

**General > Detail level** controls how much information ChatGPT Codex shows while it is working. **Coding mode** shows the specific commands ChatGPT Codex is executing. If this is more information than you need, switch to **Default** to keep your conversation cleaner.

**Personalization** works a lot like personalization in ChatGPT. You can decide whether you want ChatGPT Codex to speak to you in a friendly tone or a direct tone. You can also add custom instructions to ChatGPT Codex, the same way you can in ChatGPT.

Finally, under **Appearance > Avatar**, you can designate a friendly avatar to help you run ChatGPT Codex tasks in the background. You'll be able to keep track of its work while you focus on other windows. Drag the avatar around anywhere on your screen, and select it to be brought back to ChatGPT Codex.

You do not need to master every setting before using ChatGPT Codex. Start with these, keep your permissions conservative, and adjust as you learn what kind of work you want ChatGPT Codex to do.
