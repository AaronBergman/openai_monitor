### ChatGPT for Academic Researchers

  * Qualifying publications include arXiv, bioRxiv, or ChemRxiv papers from the past three years.

  * Identity and academic eligibility must be verified.

  * Final eligibility and collaborator rules for the new intake are to be confirmed.

  * Earlier Pro awards and grants for the existing waitlist have separate terms.




### Rosalind Academic Plan

  * The PI or designated lab lead must use a verified institutional .edu or .org account. Know Your Lab review requires an institutional biosafety contact.

  * Members need verified institutional emails matching the lead’s domain.

  * GPT‑Rosalind access requires separate trusted-access approval and activation.

  * A payment card is required for setup.

  * Premium seats, API usage and credits, and Zero Data Retention are excluded. Standard retention applies.

  * Review your accepted offer for its end date and the terms that apply afterward.

  * Universities with Enterprise agreements use their institutional access process. Edu users should confirm their route with their administrator or OpenAI account team.




### ChatGPT Edu Pro

  * Institutions arrange access through an Edu agreement with OpenAI.

  * Researchers should ask their campus administrator about seat availability and the tools included in their agreement.

  * Access to GPT‑Rosalind or Daybreak requires separate trusted-access approval.




### Researcher Access Program

  * Eligible affiliations include academic institutions, research organizations, and nonprofits conducting research. Nonprofit operational work is excluded.

  * Applicants must be in an API-supported country and submit a proposal aligned with the program’s research priorities.

  * Applications are submitted through SurveyMonkey Apply, where an account is required.

  * Credits expire after 12 months without extension. Another grant requires a new application.

  * Awards provide API credits, not cash or ChatGPT seats.

  * OpenAI usage and publication policies apply.
