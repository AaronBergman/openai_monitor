# Privacy that puts you in control

Whether you’re chatting, creating, or browsing, you’re in control of your data. We build our products and systems to protect your information, with easy-to-use settings that give you choice. 

![Blue icon of a database with a small gear, centered on a soft blue and pink gradient background.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/7hLUHakAMV3xzN4P5cE2Ax/1e70f3a5d46e864c0f98dca74a808adc/Consumer_privacy.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

## Making it easy for you to be in control of your data

## Privacy, designed into every layer

We build privacy protections into how our models are trained, how our products work, and how our teams operate.

![Privacy icon](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2OstH5IrDxmguhYl0yINQw/e6e29b99172015013bb70eea0ec50ed8/privacy.svg?w=3840&q=90)

#### We review every launch with privacy in mind

We build privacy safeguards into our models and every feature, from design to launch to post-launch monitoring.

![User lock icon](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/44W2FvT86lQ6hKrf6ntEE0/303dad1678540cc7680c7fdd3c171f5b/user-lock.svg?w=3840&q=90)

#### We reduce the amount of personal information used to train our models and make the product work

Our models are built to learn about the world, not about private individuals. We don’t seek out personal information, we don’t build profiles from public data, and we work to identify and remove personal identifiers from training sets wherever possible.

![Brain icon](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/36Jt8H5QJZ6LbJIBbFyQmN/f7e075c1c249bb99fd3ef5497c4480cb/brain.svg?w=3840&q=90)

#### Our models are trained to avoid private or sensitive information

We actively train our models to decline when asked for private information about real people—like addresses or contact information. We continuously test and update these safeguards so the models get better over time.

### Temporary chats

Sometimes you want to ask something sensitive that you don’t want saved. With temporary chats, your conversations are automatically deleted, don’t inform your ChatGPT memory, and aren’t used to train our models. You can easily start one anytime by tapping the temporary chat icon.

### Data controls

Chat data can help us improve model quality, like how to give clearer answers to questions people ask every day—but only if you want that. You can turn this setting off at any time, and any future conversations will not contribute to training. [Try now⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/#settings/DataControls>). 

### Memory controls

Memory makes ChatGPT feel more natural by remembering things you don’t want to keep reminding ChatGPT—like important people in your life, projects you’re working on, or topics you usually ask about. It’s always optional: you can review, edit, or delete saved memories, or turn memory off entirely. When it’s off, ChatGPT won’t save or reference anything from past chats. [Try now.⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Personalization>)

### Choose app access

The first time you connect an app in ChatGPT, we’ll help you understand what types of data may be collected by the third party. Developers of apps must also include clear privacy policies, collect only the minimum data they need, and be transparent about permissions. And you're always in control: you can disconnect an app at any time, and it immediately loses access. [Learn more⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/app-submission-guidelines>).

### Delete or take your data

Your data belongs to you. You can download a copy of your chats or delete them entirely whenever you choose. [Learn more⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq>).

### Ads controls

Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you, and advertisers do not have access to your chats, chat history, memories, or personal details. See our [ads principles⁠](<https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/>). 

You control how your data is used. Manage ad personalization at any time. Delete your ad data with one tap.

Ads are testing in the US for Free and Go plans. [Learn more⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt>) about ads.

### Location controls

You can choose to share your device location with ChatGPT to receive more relevant information, such as local recommendations, news, and weather. Location sharing**, including sharing precise location,** is optional and off by default until you choose to enable it. You can update this setting at any time. [_Learn more._ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search#location>)
