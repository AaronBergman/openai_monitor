# Security and controls your business can trust

Deploy AI confidently with secure workspaces, admin controls, encryption, and built-in privacy protections.

#### Your business data stays yours

  * You own and control your data

  * We don’t train our models on your data by default

  * You own your inputs and outputs




#### Manage AI across your team

  * A dedicated workspace with an admin console

  * Control who has access with user roles

  * Predictable monthly price with credit options as needed




#### Security controls built for business

  * SAML SSO and multi-factor authentication

  * SOC 2 Type 2 certified

  * Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
