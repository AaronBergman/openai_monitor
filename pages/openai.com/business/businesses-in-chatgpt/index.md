### Who can claim a business

Eligible businesses where ChatGPT is offered can claim their business, contribute current information, and access insights. Eligibility depends on factors including industry, location, and our verification and integrity checks. The [_Businesses in ChatGPT policy_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt/>) includes more details. You must own the business or be authorized to represent it to manage its profile. Business Agents are currently available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses, in the US with access expanding to more types of businesses over time.

### How to get started

Start by claiming and verifying your business, then add or update its information. Eligible Shopify merchants can connect their store and create an agent seamlessly.

### Will I show up if I don't have an agent

ChatGPT may show information about your business whether or not you have an agent when customers ask relevant questions. With a Business Agent, customers can have a conversation with your business based on information you’ve shared. The [_Business Agents policy_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/>) includes more details. 

### What claiming your business changes

Claiming lets you update your business information in ChatGPT and, if eligible, create a Business Agent. A blue check will appear on your business profile in ChatGPT to show it’s been claimed. Claiming is initially available to eligible businesses with a website.

ChatGPT continues to make independent recommendations and comparisons. Claiming does not guarantee higher placement or sales.

### How product feeds fit in

Retailers can upload their feed to share product details, pricing and availability. This allows customers to understand your offerings right in ChatGPT.
