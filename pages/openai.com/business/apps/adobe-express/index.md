# Adobe Express

Create flyers, posts, and graphics quickly.

[Add app(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/apps/adobe-express/connector_6938a61c70f88191bbdca2dedb90d08b>)[Contact sales](</contact-sales/>)

Use case: [Design](</business/apps/?tab=apps-design>)

Made by: Adobe

Website: [adobe.com(opens in a new window)](<https://adobe.com>)

## How it works

Create business and marketing visuals in ChatGPT using professional templates, customizing text, colors, and images to produce ready-to-use designs without design expertise.

### Create marketing assets faster

Create social posts, flyers, posters, and invitations from templates in minutes to support campaigns, events, or announcements.

### Keep branding consistent

Customize templates with brand colors, messaging, and imagery to keep visuals aligned across channels without needing designer updates.

### Support self-serve design

Let non-design teams create business cards, event flyers, and promos themselves to reduce design requests and speed up turnaround.

## Sample prompts

Create social ads from brief

**@Adobe Express** Create a set of 3 social ads for a gym January promo using bold typography and a consistent style.

Build internal templates

**@Adobe Express** Build a simple template pack for internal comms: announcement slide, email header, and a square post.

Design themed email assets

**@Adobe Express** Create holiday themed email header graphics that still match our brand colors and feel premium, not cheesy.
