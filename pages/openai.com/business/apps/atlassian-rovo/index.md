# Atlassian Rovo

Manage Jira and Confluence fast.

[Add app(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/apps/atlassian-rovo/connector_692de805e3ec8191834719067174a384>)[Contact sales](</contact-sales/>)

Use case: [Collaboration](</business/apps/?tab=apps-collaboration>) and [Data](</business/apps/?tab=apps-data>)

Made by: Atlassian

Website: [atlassian.com/software/rovo(opens in a new window)](<https://www.atlassian.com/software/rovo>)

## How it works

easily access the context of Jira, Confluence, and Compass and immediately take action - all within ChatGPT

### Get answers from product data instantly

Ask for metrics like daily active users or new signups and get accurate results from live Amplitude data without writing queries, reducing time to insight for product and growth teams.

### Make analytics accessible to more teams

Enable executives, marketers, and operators to explore product performance without analytics expertise, reducing dependency on analysts and speeding up everyday decision making.

### Reuse and operationalize insights

Turn natural language questions into reusable Amplitude charts and analyses, helping teams standardize metrics, track trends over time, and align faster around shared data.

## Sample prompts

Find relevant work for my work

**@Atlassian Rovo** Find the Confluence page that explains the latest incident and summarize the root cause and mitigations with links.

Convert conversation into tasks

**@Atlassian Rovo** Convert this conversation into Jira tasks with clear titles acceptance criteria and owners.

Create summary with next steps included

**@Atlassian Rovo** Create a QBR style summary using the last month of Jira delivery metrics plus the key Confluence decision notes.
