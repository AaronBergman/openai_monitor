## How it works

The easiest way to generate incredible videos with the leading state of the art model, Seedance 2 and 2.5. Turn a prompt into polished short form video. Use it for social posts, product stories, cinematic concepts, and AI-assisted creative production. \- No subscription required \- Create videos from natural-language creative direction \- Use images, video, and audio as generation references \- Edit videos \- Extend videos \- Easily download or share completed videos \- Low cost usage based pricing depends on video resolution and duration. \- Use your Infoseek account for easy access to our library of GPT apps
