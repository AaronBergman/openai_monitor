## How it works

Run real-time patient health insurance eligibility checks across 1,250+ payers using natural language. Get a patient’s coverage details, deductibles, copays, and benefits in seconds. Built-in guidance helps you troubleshoot failed checks, interpret eligibility errors, and resolve common issues quickly.
