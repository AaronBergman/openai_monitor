## How it works

Built for ChatGPT workspace owners and admins to manage members, groups, roles, permissions, GPTs, and Skills, and review Agents and audit logs. Monitor usage and billing across Chat, Codex, and Work, and manage usage limits and alerts. Available only in ChatGPT Work and Codex modes. Includes alternative-authentication setup guidance with explicit access choices, private credential entry, and approved read verification.
