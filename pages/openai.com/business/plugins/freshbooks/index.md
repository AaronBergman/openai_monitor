## How it works

Skip the spreadsheets and log-ins. With the FreshBooks app, you can ask ChatGPT about your business finances in plain language — and get clear, accurate answers pulled directly from your FreshBooks account. Know Your Profitability, On Demand — Ask ChatGPT how profitable your business was last month, last quarter, or all year. Get a clear breakdown of your revenue, expenses, and profit - no reports to run, no dashboards to dig through. Understand Where Your Money Goes — Ask ChatGPT to break your expenses down by category so you can see what's driving your costs and spot trends before they become problems. Track Revenue and Cash Position — Get a real-time read on money coming in and going out. Ask ChatGPT to compare periods, flag slow months, or summarize your overall cash position. Your Data, Just Easier to Understand — The FreshBooks app is read-only — it answers questions about your business, it doesn't make changes on your behalf. Your financial data stays exactly where it is; ChatGPT just helps you make sense of it faster.
