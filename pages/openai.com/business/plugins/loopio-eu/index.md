## How it works

Connect ChatGPT to Loopio, the AI-powered RFP and proposal response platform. Securely access and manage your content Library, and coordinate and respond to Projects with ChatGPT. \- Effortless Evaluations: Deploy the knowledge to your team's hands. Easily find approved answers using content your team already trusts. \- Faster Responses: Respond to entire RFPs, security questionnaires, and DDQs faster by responding using approved content, track progress, and review results, with visibility into the content sources answers are drawn from. \- SME Collaboration: Easily coordinate work as a team by browsing your Loopio projects, review questions and entries, and coordinating workloads across your team. \- Respond Easily: Meet your experts where they are. Refine answers and save them to project entries directly from ChatGPT. Make Loopio a native part of your AI tech stack—providing a governed backbone for your sales and response workflows. An active Loopio subscription is required. Try these prompts: \- @Loopio, list the Library entries in Loopio that need to be updated and provide suggested updated language. \- @Loopio, provide a list of Loopio Projects with deadlines in the next month. Then, provide a work breakdown between the assignees. \- @Loopio, address the technical questions I received, and draft an email response.
