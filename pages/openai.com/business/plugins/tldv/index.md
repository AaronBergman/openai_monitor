## How it works

Ask ChatGPT about any meeting tl;dv has recorded. tl;dv is the AI notetaker and meeting recorder for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. This connector gives ChatGPT read access to what tl;dv has already captured: meeting transcripts, AI notes, summaries and highlights, from your own calls and those shared across your team and company. Ask ChatGPT to pull meeting minutes, extract action items and decisions, draft follow-ups, or search your meeting history by topic, client, or speaker. You can use tl;dv to: Pull a meeting transcript: "Get the transcript from yesterday's product sync." Summarize a meeting: "What were the action items from the Acme kickoff call?" Search across meetings: "Find every meeting where we discussed the pricing change." Catch up on calls you missed: "Give me the notes from the three calls I missed while I was out." Write meeting minutes: "Turn Monday's steering committee call into formatted minutes." Jump to the highlights: "What were the key moments in the Northwind demo?" Draft a follow-up email: "Draft a follow-up to Northwind based on the demo call." Review sales calls: "What objections came up across our discovery calls this quarter?" Track customer requests: "Which customers asked for SSO in the last 60 days?" Prep for your next meeting: "What did we promise Acme on the last two calls?" Follow a deal end to end: "Summarize every call in the Contoso deal from first touch to now." Write up user interviews: "Pull the themes from this month's customer interviews." Recap a standup or team meeting: "What did the team commit to in Tuesday's standup?" Prep a 1:1 or performance review: "Summarize my 1:1s with Dani over the last quarter." Turn a webinar or training call into notes: "Key points from Thursday's onboarding session." Screen recruiting interviews: "Compare how each candidate answered the design question."
