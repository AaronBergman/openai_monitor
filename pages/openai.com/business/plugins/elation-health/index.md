## How it works

Elation Health is the EHR built for independent primary care. The Elation Health plugin for ChatGPT is in beta: connect Elation to ChatGPT to ask about your practice's schedule, patients, and insurance in plain language, and see what needs attention before each visit. Made for practices getting started. Whether you're new to Elation or just opening your doors, get to know your schedule and patient panel faster, and catch missing details while your workflows are still taking shape. Know your day before it starts. Ask who's on the schedule and which visits have a registration, insurance, or contact detail to sort out first, such as a lapsed policy or a missing preferred pharmacy, so new patients have a smooth first visit and claims start from the right information. Know where coverage stands. Review the most recent eligibility check on file for a patient's insurance before they come in. Read-only by design. The plugin reads scheduling, demographics, and insurance information from Elation, and your team makes every update in Elation. This beta covers scheduling, patient details, and coverage. Join the beta. New to Elation? Try the plugin in a sandbox environment with sample patients and schedules. Request access using this form: https://www.elationhealth.com/contact-us/openai-release/ Our team will set you up and show you how it works. Because this is a beta, features may change, and AI can make mistakes, so confirm important details in Elation.
