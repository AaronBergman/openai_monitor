## How it works

Werify gives users their own WhatsApp archive inside ChatGPT — conversations they imported from a chat export or captured from a number they connected. They can find a conversation, read its transcript a page at a time (voice notes included, as text), search inside it and list its attachments. With the user's approval it can also act: send a text message into a conversation under active capture (one message, confirmed with the user first), connect a WhatsApp number to Werify by pairing code, which only the user can complete on their phone, start or stop capturing a chat from a connected number (new messages only), and import a chat's earlier history from the phone at a price the user is shown and agrees to first.
