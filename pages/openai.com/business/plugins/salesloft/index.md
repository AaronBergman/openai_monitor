## How it works

Salesloft helps users find and inspect accounts, people, opportunities, users, and recorded conversations from their Salesloft workspace directly in ChatGPT. It supports looking up who is logged in and their team settings, searching or fetching a single account, user, opportunity, or contact by ID, and searching recorded calls and meetings to pull AI-generated summaries or a recording link. Users can also send feedback about the assistant's responses or tools back to Salesloft.
