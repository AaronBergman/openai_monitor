## How it works

Connect Klue to ChatGPT to get instant, cited answers based on trusted competitive intelligence and win-loss data. With Klue’s MCP, sellers get real-time, cited answers on positioning, objections, and what to do next in their specific deals that are in flight. PMMs can analyze win-loss patterns and competitive signals, research voice of buyer, and, where the connected tools and permissions support it, directly update content in Klue via ChatGPT. Includes the Ask Klue, Research, and Citation Instructions skills.
