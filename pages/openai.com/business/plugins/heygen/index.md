## How it works

Generate AI videos from text or any photo, and create talking avatars, AI images, voiceovers, voice clones, and dubbed videos with HeyGen in ChatGPT, for marketers, creators, educators, and small businesses. Turn an idea, script, article, document, photo, product, or existing footage into marketing videos, ads, product demos, UGC, explainers, training, tutorials, and social videos for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn. With HeyGen, you can: • Generate AI videos with HeyGen Video 1. Create videos from text, or animate any photo: products, food, rooms, and landscapes. Make cinematic scenes, short films, B-roll, animations, and product ads with or without a presenter. Create Cinematic Avatar scenes with up to three of your avatars. • Make talking-avatar videos. Have an AI avatar, AI presenter, or spokesperson deliver your script or audio for announcements, sales outreach, onboarding, HR and compliance training, courses, presentations, and customer messages, and direct the gestures, expressions, and voice. Add B-roll, music, captions, and motion graphics, or export the avatar on a transparent background to overlay on your own content. Make video podcasts and interviews with two AI hosts. • Turn any content into a finished video. Start from a topic, website, blog post, article, PowerPoint, PDF, or document and get a multi-scene narrated video with B-roll, captions, music, and on-screen graphics, then refine it in chat. Make faceless explainers, motion-graphics videos with no presenter, story videos, narrated picture stories, and slideshows. • Create your avatar. Make a Digital Twin from a short video, a photo avatar from a single image, or an AI avatar from a text description, then give it new outfits and backgrounds from a description, a style template, or a themed look pack, so you can make videos without filming. • Create voices and voiceovers. Clone your voice instantly from one recording, or train a professional voice clone from 20+ minutes of audio. Generate AI voiceovers, text-to-speech (TTS) narration, voicemail greetings, and guided meditations in many voices and languages, design a custom voice from a description, re-voice any recording with an AI voice changer (speech-to-speech), and add music and sound effects. • Make a photo talk. Turn a photo or portrait of a person into a talking, lip-synced video. • Create AI images. Generate any image, art, or wallpaper from a prompt or reference, or edit a photo: upscale it, change or expand the background, relight it, or color grade it. With HeyGen Image, put yourself or your avatar in any scene for AI headshots and profile pictures. • Make cartoons, birthday videos, and slideshows. Create animated shorts; birthday, wedding, Christmas, and holiday videos, Santa messages, and video invitations; bedtime stories for kids; and carousels and photo slideshows. Bring old photos back to life and make memorial tributes. • Make UGC and social ads. Create AI UGC ads, product reviews, tutorials, try-on hauls, unboxings, website and app promos, e-commerce product ads, and ASMR product videos from your product photos or website. • Run a video series. Write video scripts and hooks, and plan and produce a recurring series for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram, or LinkedIn on a schedule. • Translate and dub videos. Use AI dubbing and video and audio translation into 175+ languages and dialects, including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic, with voice cloning, lip-sync, and subtitles. Lip-sync any video to new audio to replace or fix lines. • Turn long videos into short clips. AI clipping finds the best moments in a podcast, webinar, interview, or livestream and cuts them into vertical clips for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Connect your HeyGen account to get started. Generations use credits from your HeyGen plan.
