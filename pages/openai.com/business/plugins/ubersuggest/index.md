## How it works

Ubersuggest is a free SEO research app that helps you quickly explore keyword ideas and website SEO metrics directly in ChatGPT. You can use the app to: Find keyword ideas and related search terms for any topic See estimated search volume, keyword difficulty, and CPC Get a high-level SEO overview for a website, including estimated traffic, keyword count, and backlinks The goal of the app is to make SEO research fast and conversational. It provides lightweight, easy-to-understand data inside ChatGPT, so you can validate ideas, explore topics, or analyze a site without leaving the conversation. When you want deeper analysis, full keyword lists, historical data, or detailed reports, the app links you to Ubersuggest’s free account experience, where you can continue exploring at no cost. No purchase is required to get started. This app is ideal for marketers, founders, content creators, and anyone who wants quick SEO insights before deciding what to research further.
