### Which plugins are available for my team to use?

Availability depends on your plan, workspace permissions, region and the plugin. Check each listing to see where it works, which accounts it requires and what setup is needed.

### Who can enable plugins for our organization?

Workspace admins manage which plugins and connected apps are available. On eligible plans, access can be limited by role or group. Some connections require admin setup or approval before users can connect their accounts. [Learn more⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11509118>).

### Can we control how plugins access our business data?

Yes. Admins manage plugin and app availability in your workspace. Where supported, they can limit connected apps to read-only access, enable specific actions, and require approval before ChatGPT reads information or makes changes. Enterprise and Edu also offer role-based access controls to limit who can use each app. Access remains subject to the permissions of your connected accounts and data sources. Learn more about [admin controls and permissions⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11509118>).

For ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, and Edu, OpenAI does not use data accessed from connected apps to train models by default. Data shared with connected services is subject to those services’ terms and privacy policies. Learn more about [data privacy⁠](<https://openai.com/business-data/>).

### Can we build plugins for our organization?

Yes. Build a plugin for your team’s workflows and tools. Workspace admins can publish it to permitted roles, subject to workspace settings. Only people signed in to that workspace can access it. [Learn how to build and share plugins.⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/plugins>)

### Can we build plugins for our customers?

Yes. Build a plugin that brings your product’s tools, data, and interactive experiences into ChatGPT. You can submit it for review for publication in the plugin directory, so customers can discover and use your product where they already work. [Explore the developer guides⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://developers.openai.com/plugins>).
