## How it works

Work with your HubSpot data to uncover patterns, prepare reports, and answer everyday questions about your pipeline or customer base.

## Common use cases

  * ### Answer everyday questions with chat

Use chat to quickly answer simple questions, such as “Give me a breakdown of open vs. closed deals,” using your HubSpot data directly.

HubSpot Give me a pipeline health check including open deals by stage, biggest risks, and which deals need attention today.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=HubSpot+Give+me+a+pipeline+health+check+including+open+deals+by+stage%2C+biggest+risks%2C+and+which+deals+need+attention+today.>)

  * ### Conduct complex analysis with deep research

Use deep research to run more advanced analyses with your HubSpot context, such as identifying why deals were lost last quarter or which segments are trending up.

HubSpot Analyze deals lost last quarter and summarize the top loss reasons by segment, including patterns in notes or missing fields.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=HubSpot+Analyze+deals+lost+last+quarter+and+summarize+the+top+loss+reasons+by+segment%2C+including+patterns+in+notes+or+missing+fields.>)

  * ### Trusted results aligned to HubSpot permissions

Access is based on a user’s HubSpot permissions, so they only see data they're authorized to view, excluding Sensitive Data Properties and Highly Sensitive Data (e.g. PHI).

HubSpot For my next customer call, pull contact/company history plus recent emails and notes, then suggest a short agenda and smart questions.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=HubSpot+For+my+next+customer+call%2C+pull+contact%2Fcompany+history+plus+recent+emails+and+notes%2C+then+suggest+a+short+agenda+and+smart+questions.>)
