## How it works

Quartr helps users research public companies by searching company profiles, events, conferences, documents, transcripts, summaries, and financials. It also lets users manage Quartr research assets such as watchlists, keyword alerts, saved clips folders, workspaces, and saved search filters through ChatGPT.
