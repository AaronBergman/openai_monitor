## How it works

Sandstone helps in-house legal teams connect every person, company, and document tied to a piece of legal work into one living system, so that when a request comes in — from Slack or Teams, email, or wherever the business already works — your team isn't starting from scratch. Sandstone's Plugin allows you to access this context directly from ChatGPT. It's Legal Relationship Management, built in.
