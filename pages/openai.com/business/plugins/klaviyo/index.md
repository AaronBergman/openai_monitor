## How it works

Bring your Klaviyo data into the chats where you already plan campaigns. Connect Klaviyo in a few clicks. From the chat, you can: \- See how last week's email and SMS campaigns performed, with open rates, click rates, conversion rate, and attributed revenue. \- Break down any flow message by message to find where people drop off. \- Ask why performance changed and get next steps for timing, testing, and audiences. \- Draft email and SMS campaigns, then send them once you confirm. \- Create flows and build lists and segments from your customer data. Every answer comes from your live Klaviyo data, and campaigns send only after you confirm.
