## How it works

Connect ChatGPT directly to your WhatsApp Business API to orchestrate messaging, build trigger-based workflows, and deploy customer-facing AI agents. With the Peach MCP integration, ChatGPT gets administrative and operational tools for your authenticated WhatsApp Business channel: read conversations and message history, send templates and app messages, manage media, schedule targeted broadcasts, build AI support agents, react to Shopify events, route conversations, manage official Meta WhatsApp templates, and keep contacts and pipeline stages up to date from WhatsApp interactions. Turn your WhatsApp number into an active, automated operating system managed directly inside ChatGPT.
