## How it works

Bring Responsive project work into ChatGPT. Proposal teams can create and monitor response projects, identify unanswered or unreviewed questions, draft and refine responses, and coordinate review across RFPs, proposals, DDQs, and security questionnaires. SMEs can access assigned work, review supporting information, contribute their expertise, and move collaboration forward in ChatGPT. Responsive keeps the work connected to live project context, existing permissions, and approved knowledge while remaining the system of record.
