## How it works

TopCounsel helps users find, compare, and diligence law firms and individual lawyers using anonymized peer signal from The L Suite community of in-house counsel. It supports outside-counsel discovery by practice area, geography, industry, matter type, and named-firm reputation while preserving member anonymity.
