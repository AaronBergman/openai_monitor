## How it works

Source-grounded legal workflows for litigators, with shared daily-practice tools.

## Common use cases

  * ### Check the motion's authorities

Check the authorities cited in opposing counsel's motion against the supplied source materials.

@LegalQuants Litigation Check every authority in their motion. The authorities I have are in this folder.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=%40LegalQuants+Litigation+Check+every+authority+in+their+motion.+The+authorities+I+have+are+in+this+folder.>)

  * ### Pressure-test an opposing motion

Assess an opposing motion against your documents to identify weaknesses and issues for review.

@LegalQuants Litigation Pressure-test the other side's motion against our documents.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=%40LegalQuants+Litigation+Pressure-test+the+other+side%27s+motion+against+our+documents.>)

  * ### Prepare a deposition outline

Turn the matter record into a witness deposition outline supported by exhibits.

@LegalQuants Litigation Prepare a deposition outline for our witness from the matter record, with exhibits.

[(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/?q=%40LegalQuants+Litigation+Prepare+a+deposition+outline+for+our+witness+from+the+matter+record%2C+with+exhibits.>)
