## How it works

Connect to ServiceNow directly in ChatGPT via ServiceNow Action Fabric. Run governed cross-departmental workflows and actions across IT, HR, Customer Service, Security, Risk, and more, all while respecting your existing ServiceNow permissions and security controls.
