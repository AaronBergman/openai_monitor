## How it works

Use Subsalt to explore authorized database metadata and synthetic data, manage workspaces and cohorts, and run privacy-preserving Secure Compute analyses. Subsalt is for data and analytics teams working with governed data. Sign in with your Subsalt account; available data and actions always follow your Subsalt permissions.
