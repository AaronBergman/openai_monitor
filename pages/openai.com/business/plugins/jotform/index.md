## How it works

Create, edit, and analyze forms directly inside ChatGPT. Simply describe what you need, and Jotform generates a complete form that you can refine through conversation. Build request forms, application forms, surveys, evaluation forms, report forms, consent and acknowledgement forms, lead generation forms, feedback forms, quizzes, registration forms, RSVP forms, order forms, appointment booking forms, and more. Add, remove, and reorder fields, customize layouts and themes, then share your form and start collecting responses. View submissions in real time, filter responses using natural language, summarize feedback, compare results, identify trends, uncover recurring themes, and generate actionable insights from your form data. Key Features: \- Create forms instantly from natural language prompts \- Build request, application, survey, evaluation, report, consent, lead generation, feedback, quiz, registration, and order forms \- Add, remove, reorder, and customize form fields through conversation \- Update layouts, themes, settings, and form design \- List, search, and manage your forms \- View and filter submissions by date, status, keywords, or field values \- Analyze responses with AI to summarize results, identify trends, and generate insights Create forms for every use case, including: \- Customer feedback surveys \- Event registration and RSVP forms \- Job application and onboarding forms \- Contact and lead generation forms \- Order and payment forms \- Quizzes and assessments \- Appointment booking forms \- Internal requests and approval forms
