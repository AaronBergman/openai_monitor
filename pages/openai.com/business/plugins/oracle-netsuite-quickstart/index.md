## How it works

Spend less time managing your finances and more time building your company. QuickStart brings your accounting, banking, subscriptions, SaaS metrics, and reporting into ChatGPT so you can understand what’s happening in your business, get answers from your financial data, and take action without jumping between tools. Built for early-stage tech startups, with a path to NetSuite as you grow.
