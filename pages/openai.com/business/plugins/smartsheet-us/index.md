## How it works

Make Smartsheet a native part of your AI workflow. Ask your AI to analyze project timelines, create graphs to summarize your sheet information, all through conversation. The Smartsheet connector eliminates manual data entry and repetitive updates, letting you focus on decisions while AI handles the details. Transform how you manage projects with AI that understands your Smartsheet data and can act on it.
