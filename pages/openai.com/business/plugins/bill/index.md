## How it works

Connect BILL to ChatGPT for instant visibility into your company’s payables, receivables, and spend. Search bills, payments, invoices, and card transactions, and summarize what’s due, outstanding, or trending. Access is read-only and runs as each signed-in user: same data, same access, same permissions as BILL itself.
