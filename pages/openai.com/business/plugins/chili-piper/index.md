## How it works

Connect ChatGPT to your Chili Piper account to review and manage meetings, lead routing, and scheduling in plain language. Ask questions like "How many meetings did each rep get last week?", "Why didn't this lead book?", or "Why does this rep have no availability?" and get answers from your live Chili Piper data. What you can do: • Meetings: list, export, and inspect meetings, including how each one was booked and routed. Mark no-shows or cancel. • Routing: review Concierge, Distro, and Handoff routers, rules, and distributions. Audit for coverage gaps and adjust round-robin weights. • Scheduling: check rep and team availability, and manage meeting types and scheduling links. • Users and teams: look up users, manage workspace and team membership, and onboard or offboard reps. • Chat AI: review conversation logs and routing outcomes. Includes built-in skills for common admin workflows: meeting diagnostics, routing audits, no-show analysis, availability checks, and user onboarding and offboarding. Skills that make changes show you a plan and ask for confirmation first. Requires a Chili Piper account. Sign in with a Chili Piper Admin login.
