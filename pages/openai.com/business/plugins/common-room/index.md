## How it works

Embed complete buyer intelligence directly within ChatGPT, and act on it. Research accounts and contacts, surface buying signals, and browse activity history - all through natural language. Find people and companies that aren't in your workspace yet in Prospector, build prospect lists by industry, size, tech stack, or location, and keep them in segments that stay current. When your admin allows it, enroll up to 10 contacts at a time, each with a message you review, in Outreach, Salesloft, HubSpot, Gong, Apollo, Groove, Smartlead or Zoom Engage sequences or Salesforce campaigns, push them to a Zoom dialer call list, or export them to your CRM, then check the result for every contact. Every result is grounded in your CRM fields, scores, and signals - so you always know what's actually happening in your accounts. You can use Common Room to: \- Account Research: "Tell me about Datadog and create a plan on how to approach the account." \- Contact Lookup: "Research jane.doe@company.com using Common Room." \- Prospecting: "Find SaaS companies in the US using Snowflake with 200-1000 employees." \- Segment and Role Filtering: "Show me all Economic Buyers in my 'Champions changing jobs' segment." \- High-Intent Contacts: "Find contacts at ICP companies who have visited our pricing page this week." \- Website Visitor Tracking: "Who visited our pricing page in the last 7 days?" \- Segment Building: "Create a segment of ICP accounts that visited the pricing page in the last 30 days." \- List Qualification: "Here are 20 webinar attendees and their companies. Which fit our ICP?" \- Sequence Enrollment: "Enroll the Acme champions in our Q4 expansion sequence — show me the messages first." \- Call Lists: "Load this week's high-intent contacts with phone numbers into the SDR call list." \- CRM Export: "Export the new champions we found today to Salesforce." \- Action Results: "Did all of yesterday's enrollments go through? Which failed and why?" \- Call Follow-up: "Log a demo activity for everyone on today's Figma call." \- Team Activity: "How many contacts did each team send to sequences this month?" Outreach, call list and CRM actions are off until a workspace admin turns them on, and ChatGPT asks before each one.
