# Adobe Photoshop

Edit, stylize, and refine images.

[Add plugin(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69312da8e4dc81919370cb86fd172b6c>)[Contact sales](</contact-sales/>)

Use case: [Design](</business/plugins/?tab=apps-design>)

Made by: OpenAI

Website: [adobe.com(opens in a new window)](<https://adobe.com>)

## How it works

Edit, refine, and stylize images in ChatGPT by removing backgrounds, adjusting color and lighting, and applying effects, then continue in Photoshop for full creative control.

### Accelerate image editing

Edit and refine images faster using background removal, color adjustments, and creative effects to support quicker asset creation.

### Maintain visual consistency

Adjust color, tone, and style across images before publishing to web, social, or ads to help keep visuals consistent.

### Reduce creative bottlenecks

Enable non-designers to handle routine image edits themselves, reducing design requests and supporting faster turnaround.

## Sample prompts

Remove image backgrounds cleanly

**@Adobe Photoshop** Remove the background from the latest product images and make them consistent for ecommerce: clean edges, true-to-life color, and transparent PNGs.

Refine images for consistent branding

**@Adobe Photoshop** Refine this set of headshots so they look consistent and on-brand. Adjust lighting, color balance, cropping, and backgrounds to create a polished, professional, and natural look.

Adapt images for multiple channels

**@Adobe Photoshop** Adapt this image for use across multiple channels like social, web, email, and ads. Resize and crop thoughtfully, adjusting composition as needed so the image looks intentional and high-quality in each format.
