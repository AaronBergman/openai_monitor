## How it works

For authorized healthcare staff using Luma Health to manage administrative patient-access workflows: search patient records, review appointments, and find providers in their connected organization. Available actions depend on the connected account and server tool set. Changes to appointments and records require explicit user intent. Requires a Luma Health account and sign-in through the connected organization. This integration supports healthcare administration and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
