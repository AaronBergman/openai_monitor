## How it works

Clinical guidance with sources from UpToDate Expert AI, displayed in ChatGPT. Clinicians can explicitly ask UpToDate a clinical question, view the streamed answer and citations, ask a clinical follow-up in the same conversation, and open the saved conversation in Expert AI. Requires a valid UpToDate subscription, an eligible account, and ChatGPT connection access enabled. Existing answers can be explained or summarized without starting another consultation. This integration does not prescribe, place orders, or access an electronic health record.
