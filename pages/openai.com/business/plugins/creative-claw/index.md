## How it works

Create AI videos, ads, images, voiceovers, and cloned voices directly in ChatGPT. Turn scripts and product photos into product ads, animate still images, or build multi-scene films. Creative Claw uses pay-as-you-go credits. No Creative Claw subscription is required. Make product demos, explainer videos, creator-style UGC ads, Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts. Turn longer videos into shorter clips, cut and reorder selected moments, reframe footage for vertical formats, and add captions. Plan storyboards, reuse character references across scenes, and assemble clips into a finished video. Turn text into speech for narration, dialogue, and multilingual voiceovers. Clone your own voice, or another speaker’s voice with their explicit permission, using ElevenLabs. Save and reuse that voice across videos and projects. Generate music and sound effects for your productions. Generate and edit images, create product photos and campaign visuals, or combine reference images to guide composition, style, and character appearance. Choose video models including Gemini Omni, Seedance 2.5, Seedance Mini, MiniMax H3 Max, H3 Max Fast, and FLUX 3; image models including Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro, and Seedream 5 Pro; and speech options including ElevenLabs, xAI, and MiniMax. Model availability and supported settings vary. Save and reuse your media assets across projects. Request credit estimates before generating. Generation and editing consume credits, with costs depending on the model and settings. Results, including character consistency and voice similarity, can vary.
