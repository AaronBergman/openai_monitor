## How it works

Connect Crayon to ChatGPT and get competitive intelligence exactly when you need it, without switching tabs or digging through docs. Once connected, ChatGPT can pull your team’s competitive battlecards and latest competitor insights in real time. Ask about a competitor, prep for a call, or get help handling objections using live Crayon data. The result: high quality competitive answers where your team is already working.
