## How it works

Connect to IMO Health's MCP server for clinical terminology normalization and knowledge-graph queries. Normalize clinical terms across Problem, Procedure, Medication, and Lab domains; retrieve cross-vocabulary mappings (ICD-10-CM, SNOMED CT, CPT, HCPCS, RxNorm); explore Problem-domain refinements and hierarchies for diagnosis specificity; and navigate cross-domain and related-problem relationships via GraphQL. Available tools and datasets depend on the connected server and account permissions. Use only documented clinical details when refining a diagnosis.
