## How it works

View top cryptocurrency prices live, crypto charts, market cap, and trading volume. Discover today’s trending coins, top crypto gainers and losers in the market such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, BNB, Hyperliquid, and more. You can also compare them side by side in a table.
