## How it works

From landing pages and presentations to launch graphics, Superdesign turns your ideas into polished visuals you can explore and refine with ChatGPT. Approve slide outlines, compare design directions on an infinite browser canvas, and keep iterating—all grounded in your brand and existing design system.
