## How it works

Publish the pages, reports, dashboards, and other artifacts you create with ChatGPT and get a link to share them. Spacefast lets you update existing sites, manage access, connect custom domains, and view or restore earlier versions. Connect your Spacefast account to manage your team's Spaces from the conversation.
