## How it works

Xmind for ChatGPT turns your ideas into mind maps — just by chatting. Describe a project, goal, or topic in plain words, and Xmind organizes it into a structured visual map so you can see the connections at a glance. One click opens it in the full Xmind app for deeper editing. Great for planning, learning, note-taking, and brainstorming.
