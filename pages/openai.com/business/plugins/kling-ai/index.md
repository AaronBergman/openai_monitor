## How it works

Turn every idea into a striking image or video. Kling AI is an AI image and video generator for creators. In local Codex tasks, use natural language for text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, image-to-video, reusable character, animal and prop Elements, motion control from a video or saved motion, and single-shot or multi-shot video through the official Kling CLI. Create posters, illustrations, portraits, product photos, e-commerce hero images, product listing images, product detail images, ad creatives, product showcase videos, marketing clips, e-commerce short videos, social media videos, and cinematic content for Taobao, Tmall, JD.com, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Kuaishou, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Amazon, Shopify, and more. Paste or attach reference images in the conversation; Codex handles the CLI runtime, browser OAuth, local image paths, credit checks, generation submission, progress tracking, task status, and result retrieval. Requires a local Codex environment with Node.js 18+, Kling CLI 0.2.0+, and shell access. Supports explicit asset uploads, account switching, tool discovery, and transparent, user-authorized issue feedback. Looking for alternatives like Hailuo AI, Seedance, Vidu, Jimeng, Runway, Pika, Sora, or Veo? Use Kling AI (KLING / Kling / 可灵) to build a high-quality local creative workflow.
