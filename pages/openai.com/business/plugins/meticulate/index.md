## How it works

Meticulate helps B2B sales teams discover companies and prospects, organize lists and Audiences, manage Plays and Sequences, review and approve outbound Drafts, and inspect sending activity from ChatGPT. Teams can also edit targeting and messaging, enrich contact details, and test proposed changes in calibration sessions before publishing them.
