## How it works

Assort Health brings referral data into ChatGPT for healthcare organizations. Ask why a referring practice's volume dropped, or where referrals are leaking. Add the context that lives outside Assort, like payer contracts, provider rosters, capacity plans, and market data, and get it reconciled against what's happening at your front door. Rank outreach targets by insurance overlap and geography, then draft the letters and fax them. Turn conversion, specialty mix, and payer trends into a QBR with the narrative already written. Call center, intake, and payments data are coming soon. Assort Health for ChatGPT is in early access and requires an Assort Health account and an OpenAI for Healthcare workspace. To get access, contact your Assort Health representative or book a demo at assorthealth.com.
