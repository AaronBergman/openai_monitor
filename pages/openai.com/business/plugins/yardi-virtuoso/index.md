## How it works

Yardi Virtuoso helps investment, property, and asset management professionals explore Yardi information through ChatGPT. Ask about financial reports, budgets, receivables, leases, debt, vendors, and payables using natural language. Available data and tools depend on your connected Yardi account and permissions.
