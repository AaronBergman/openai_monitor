# PandaDoc

Send documents for signature

## How it works

Create any agreement in ChatGPT and send it for e-signature without leaving the conversation. Describe what you need, watch it take shape, add your recipients, and send. Your AI draft becomes a polished, signature-ready agreement automatically.
