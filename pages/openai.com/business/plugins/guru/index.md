## How it works

Connect ChatGPT to your company's knowledge in Guru. Search verified Guru cards and connected sources, and get answers that cite the source documents they came from. Results respect your existing Guru permissions, so you only see content your account can already access. Built for teams that keep policies, processes and product information in Guru. Requires a Guru account.
