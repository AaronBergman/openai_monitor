## How it works

Real-time prices, market cap, and volume for millions of crypto assets tracked by CoinMarketCap, from major cryptocurrencies to long-tail DEX tokens across every major chain. Beyond prices: technical analysis (RSI, MACD, moving averages, Fibonacci levels, pivot points), on-chain metrics like holder distribution and supply breakdowns, and global market signals including BTC dominance, Fear & Greed Index, altcoin season gauge, leverage stats, and ETF flows. Discover trending crypto narratives, upcoming macro events that move markets, breaking crypto news, and semantic search across crypto concepts, whitepapers, and project documentation.
