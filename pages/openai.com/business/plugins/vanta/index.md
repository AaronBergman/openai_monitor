## How it works

Vanta helps users review compliance tests, controls, evidence, policies, risks, vendors, audits, personnel, access reviews, and vulnerabilities in ChatGPT. Supported workflows include managing tests and controls, uploading and evaluating evidence, creating and deleting policies, updating risk and vendor records, configuring SLAs, and managing AI governance records, questionnaires, and knowledge base resources. Users can also create tickets in connected services and manage privacy requests. Policy approval and privacy workflows can send notifications, and knowledge base resources can be published to the Trust Center. Available tools depend on the connected organization's enabled features and the user's permissions.
