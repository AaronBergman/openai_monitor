## How it works

A journey-oriented NGS workbench for FASTQ QC, bulk RNA-seq, and single-cell RNA-seq. Focused skills establish the user's starting point, design toward a scientific outcome, bind that design to live Nextflow/nf-core or Snakemake capabilities, preserve checksum-bound native approval, and interpret completed results from file-backed evidence.
