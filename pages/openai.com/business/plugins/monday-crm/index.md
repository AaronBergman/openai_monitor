## How it works

Stay on top of leads, deals, and accounts with monday CRM in ChatGPT and Codex. Get pipeline insights, surface AI suggestions for follow-ups and blockers, and prepare customer communications grounded in your CRM. You stay in control: changes and messages require your approval.
