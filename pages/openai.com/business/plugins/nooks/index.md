## How it works

Bring Nooks into ChatGPT so your revenue team can act on real execution data without pulling exports or switching tools. Query tasks, prospects, sequences, email activity, call transcripts, and rep metadata. Nooks is a revenue agent platform where AI agents and reps work in the same system, giving teams a direct line from agent activity to meetings booked, pipeline generated, and revenue closed.
