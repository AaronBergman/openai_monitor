## How it works

Cargo is GTM infrastructure for AI assistants. Connect this MCP server and ChatGPT can operate your Cargo workspace: discover and run actions across 130+ integrations, execute tools and agents, query CRM and revenue models, and read the playbooks and ICP context your team already wrote. Sign in with OAuth — no API key to paste. Your session binds to one workspace, so ChatGPT sees your integrations, your models, and your credits. What you can do from chat: • Search the action catalog and see credit cost before anything runs • Enrich people and companies, look up CRM records, and call native platform ops • Run a single action or a batch across many records • Query workspace models (selects, joins, aggregates) • Read workspace context — ICP definitions, playbooks, rubrics — before guessing Ask in plain English. ChatGPT finds the right action, confirms cost when it matters, and brings results back into the conversation. Best for RevOps, sales ops, and GTM engineers who want research-to-action without leaving chat. Building multi-step workflows or workspace-as-code stays in the Cargo CLI — this connector is the runtime for in-conversation work. Learn more: https://www.getcargo.ai/mcp
