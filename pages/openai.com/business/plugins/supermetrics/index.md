## How it works

Connect ChatGPT directly to your marketing and analytics data with Supermetrics. Query data from platforms like Google Ads, Meta Ads, Instagram, Google Analytics, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok, Shopify, Amazon and more. The Supermetrics MCP server enables secure, real-time access to your marketing data sources, allowing you to: -Retrieve campaign performance metrics instantly -Compare cross-channel results -Generate marketing reports with live data -Analyze trends and anomalies -Create new marketing campaigns or edit existing ones Built on Supermetrics’ trusted data infrastructure, this integration ensures accurate, governed, and up-to-date marketing data inside ChatGPT.
