## How it works

Connect ChatGPT to Google Ads, Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Advertising, GA4 and 200+ marketing data sources with Supermetrics — to both analyze your data and take action on your campaigns. Beyond reporting, you can create, edit and optimize campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft Advertising, Snapchat and ChatGPT Ads directly from chat — adjust budgets and bids, pause or enable campaigns, update targeting and manage creatives. The Supermetrics MCP server gives secure, real-time access so you can ask for things like: \- "How did our Google Ads campaigns perform last month vs the month before?" \- "Pause every campaign with a cost per conversion above €50." \- "Reallocate budget to my highest-performing campaigns." \- "Launch a new Meta campaign targeting our retargeting audience." \- "Compare cost per conversion across Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn this quarter." \- "Which campaigns are wasting spend right now — and cut their budgets 20%." \- "Build a live dashboard of our paid media performance I can share with the team." Works with the ad, analytics, CRM and e-commerce platforms your team already uses, including Google Search Console, Shopify, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Amazon Ads, Pinterest Ads, Snapchat and Reddit Ads. Turn an analysis into a live dashboard in Supermetrics Studio: a shareable link that re-queries your data on every view, so the numbers are always current. Built-in skills guide ChatGPT through common marketing workflows: live dashboards, weekly performance reviews, wasted-spend audits, budget pacing, creative fatigue, funnel and tracking audits, ecommerce ROAS, B2B pipeline, SEO and content, event ROI and executive reporting. Reading data requires connecting each account once. Campaign changes are possible only on ad accounts where your team has enabled write access, and account owners choose which changes need human approval before they apply. New campaigns are created paused. Data is fetched from each platform's API through Supermetrics when you ask. Supermetrics is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms it connects to.
