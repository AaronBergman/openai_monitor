## How it works

PitchBook provides structured access to private capital market data across companies, investors, funds, deals, limited partners, and people. It returns normalized fields such as firmographics, financing rounds, deal terms, ownership information, financials, fund performance metrics when available, LP commitments, investor portfolios, and team details with associated source metadata. Use this connector when a query requires factual private market information or retrieval of structured data related to private companies, investment activity, or market participants. It supports queries that involve identifying entities, summarizing historical activity, or assembling datasets for further analysis.
