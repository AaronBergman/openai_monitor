## How it works

Search over 400 million state and federal cases in plain English to quickly and easily locate on-point cases, view case details, and access relevant information. Identify key participants (parties, judges, and counsel) and review the docket history to understand what happened in any case. To use the plugin, users must have an active subscription with UniCourt. For access, please contact the UniCourt team at sales@unicourt.com.
