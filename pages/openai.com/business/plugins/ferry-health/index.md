## How it works

Ferry partners with providers and insurers to deliver AI-powered concierge healthcare navigation. Connect your Ferry account to see how many appointment tasks are in each status across the organizations you can access, and get a clickable Ferry link for every task in a given status so you can jump straight to the work that needs attention. The plugin is read-only.
