## How it works

AI-assisted coding: the AI suggests codes, and a qualified coder makes every decision. Turn a de-identified chart into coding suggestions, each backed by a quote from the chart. What it does: \- ICD-10-CM codes: splits the chart into visits and codes each one. You get a main diagnosis plus the other diagnoses the visit supports. \- HCC risk adjustment: codes each eligible visit with MEAT evidence and maps the codes to CMS-HCC v24, v28 and RxHCC, year by year. \- Home health (PDGM): finds the main diagnosis and its PDGM group, then lists the other diagnoses in order. \- Chart summary: conditions, medications, results, allergies and a visit timeline, each linked to its page and line. No codes. \- Coder review: accept or reject any suggested code. Your name is recorded with the decision. Good to know: \- Every code has a quote that is really in the chart. No quote, no code. \- Send de-identified charts only. Each chart is checked for patient details such as names, phone numbers and dates of birth. If any are found, the chart is refused and you are told what to remove and where. Dates: \- Accepted: dates of service, visits, admissions, discharges, labs and signatures, in full, for example 'Date of service: 2025-05-12'. HCC risk adjustment needs the full date of service. \- Refused: a full date of birth or date of death. Write the year only, for example 'DOB: 1954'. \- A visit with only a year is not eligible for HCC, and the result tells you why. \- Results always show the year only, for example '2025, encounter 1'. Sign in with your Gwen account (gwen.penguinai.co). Each request uses Gwen credits. This is AI-assisted coding, not automated coding. Results are suggestions for a qualified coder to review and accept or reject. They are not billing decisions.
