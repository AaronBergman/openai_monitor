## How it works

K Health’s clinical AI runs a patient-facing, record-aware intake before every visit, capturing the complaint and related symptoms. Clinicians then get a pre-populated chart, schedule, visit history, and preferred pharmacy in a single view, which is accessible through this plugin. Access is read-only, requires a work account, is limited to scheduled sessions, and must be verified.
