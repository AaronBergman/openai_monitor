## How it works

Work with live HighLevel business data from ChatGPT and Codex. Find and summarize contacts, opportunities, conversations, tasks, and appointments. Create and update records, send messages, enroll contacts in workflows, manage content, and use other available HighLevel operations, including supported commerce and account-management actions. Connect through OAuth using your HighLevel agency account and work in the sub-accounts authorized for the connection. Access to multiple sub-accounts is available when enabled for your installation. Available actions depend on your granted permissions, account entitlements, and the active HighLevel operation catalog. Identify the intended records and sub-account before making changes. Preview a change when requested and confirm consequential actions before execution. Results reflect the service response; missing permissions or unavailable operations are reported.
