## How it works

AI Graphic Design is powered by Superdesign. Create polished posters, social graphics and campaign assets; design coherent slides, presentations, pitch decks and PPT-style decks; or use a persistent Moodboard project to collect AI-generated images and visual references on the Superdesign infinite canvas. Reopen the canvas whenever you want to view or reuse those assets, and create gallery or exhibition-style webpages from selected material when requested.
