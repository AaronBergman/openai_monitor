## How it works

Use Upwork from your AI agent to hire freelancers and find freelance work. Clients can search freelancer profiles, draft and publish job posts, and review proposals. Freelancers can search jobs, check their dashboard and Connects balance, and draft proposals. Connects to your Upwork account through the Upwork MCP server. Actions that change your account, such as publishing a job post or submitting a proposal, require your confirmation. In order to use this app, you need to have an active Upwork account. Please contact our support team (https://support.upwork.com) for more details. Get started: sign up or log in at https://www.upwork.com, then install this plugin and connect it to your Upwork account when prompted. A getting-started guide is available at https://www.upwork.com/ai/mcp. Help and documentation: https://support.upwork.com
