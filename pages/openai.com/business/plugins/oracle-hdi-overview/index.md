## How it works

Explore the Oracle Health Data Intelligence (HDI) overview, learn about its published capabilities, and find contact and demo information. The overview is shown once per conversation and can be reopened on request. Follow-up product questions use web search when available. This plugin provides public product information; it does not access patient records or execute the clinical, research, or operational workflows described on the overview page. The following intended-use statement describes the broader Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector. The overview plugin packaged here supplies public product information only. Intended Use Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector enables authorized users to access Oracle Health agents, tools, and data through permitted third-party AI agent clients using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is intended to support retrospective operational, clinical research, policy, population-health, and life-sciences research and analytics workflows, including cohort creation, care-gap analysis, patient summarization, patient prioritization, research exploration, and related analytic tasks. Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is an integration layer intended to facilitate adjunctive analytic support. Oracle Health agents accessed through Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector are not intended to replace clinical judgment, provide treatment or diagnostic recommendations, inform real-time clinical care, or support clinical decision-making for an individual patient. Indications for Use Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector is indicated for use by authorized analytics, research, informatics, population health, care management, and operational users who are entitled to Oracle Health Data Intelligence capabilities and who access those capabilities through permitted third-party AI client interfaces. Users may request analytic assistance, cohort or concept construction, data exploration, subject to customer entitlements, data rights, access controls, and jurisdiction-specific approvals. Disclaimer Oracle is not responsible for outputs or other content generated by third-party products accessed through or made available by Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector’s Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration layer. Oracle disclaims any and all liability arising from use of third-party products accessed through Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector, and Customer acknowledges that such use involves interacting with the applicable third party, not Oracle. Oracle does not endorse any third-party products accessed through Oracle Health Data Intelligence Connector or review third-party content for compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including medical device laws.
