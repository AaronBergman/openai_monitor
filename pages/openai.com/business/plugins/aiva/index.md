## How it works

Aiva helps clinicians get things done on ChatGPT for Healthcare! Give your clinicians real-time voice control over every IT enterprise system in your hospitals including: \- Work orders in ServiceNow: Submit an IT Service Management ticket the moment you see a problem, speeding resolution \- Policies & Procedures in Wolters Kluwer and Elsevier: Look up proprietary and licensed content real-time, improving utilization and outcomes. \- EHRs like Epic and Oracle Cerner: Voice chart across multiple rows and flowsheets in a single command, reducing latency, improving compliance, decreasing incidental overtime and increasing staff and patient satisfaction scores. Lookup vitals, labs and other information from the EHR in real-time. \- Dietary like CBORD and Computrition: Give nurses immediate access to all the dietary order information they need without having to place calls or go hunting for information, improving service while reducing distractions. \- Messaging tools like Vocera, Voalte and TigerConnect: Send messages to colleagues without having to open up different apps, facilitating communication across the hospital \- Smart Patient Rooms built with Siemens, Johnson Controls, Trane, Schneider Electric: Smart devices like lights, blinds, thermostats and TVs, freeing up more time for higher-value direct care. \- Patient experience via GetWell, Sonifi, pCare, Vibe, Aceso: Clinicians can play programming for patients like educational videos and meal information. Aiva clients have reported ROI metrics at the AHA, AMIA and AONL: \- Improve documentation timeliness by 90% \- Reduce incidental nurse overtime by 50% \- Increase Press Ganey scores on nursing-sensitive questions up to 37% View a presentation by Cedars-Sinai nurse leaders to the American Medical Informatics Association (AMIA): <p><a href="https://amia.org/education-events/education-catalog/25x5-webinar-series-reducing-burden-elevating-care-lessons">https://amia.org/education-events/education-catalog/25x5-webinar-series-reducing-burden-elevating-care-lessons</a></p> Aiva benefits for key stakeholders: A secure, HIPAA-compliant platform using API connections and ChatGPT for Healthcare as the single, conversational UI for your entire enterprise technology stack, improving utilization and effectiveness of all your systems. Clinical: An always-on assistant that eliminates all of the interfaces of the systems that detract from direct patient care. Informatics: Proven improvements in documentation latency and compliance while reducing administrative burden. Corporate: Bottom line improvement from reductions in overtime, staff turnover and documentation compliance.
