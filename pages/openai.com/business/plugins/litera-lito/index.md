## How it works

Litera's plugin brings your firm's collective knowledge and experience into ChatGPT. Ask questions in plain English and get cited answers drawn from your firm's experience data, without leaving the conversation. \- Natural language search: ask questions like "Show me M&A deals for banking clients in Japan" and get results with entity links across matters, clients, people and experience records. \- Tabular results: view paged, sortable tables from any search to quickly scan and compare results. \- Matter profiles: pull up full matter details by drilling into a specific matter from your search results. \- Client profiles: retrieve complete client profiles to understand relationship history and related experience. \- People profiles: look up individuals to find their expertise, matter history and firm roles. \- Marketing packet profiles: access full experience packets to support pitches, proposals and RFPs. \- Conversational refinement: chain any of the above together with follow-up questions to narrow down exactly what you need. All answers include citations and respect firm security permissions, ethical walls, and user entitlements, so professionals only see what they are authorised to access. Litera helps lawyers and business professionals tap into firm knowledge, Raising The Bar™ on a process that would otherwise require navigating multiple systems or relying on institutional memory.
