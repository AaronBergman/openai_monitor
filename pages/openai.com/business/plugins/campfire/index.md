## How it works

Campfire connects ChatGPT to your company accounting workspace to find bills, invoices, contracts, journal entries, and other records; analyze financial reports and budgets; inspect close tasks, attachments, and support cases; and search Campfire help documentation. With the necessary access, you can create, update, and delete supported accounting records and manage budgets, tags, and custom fields. Existing permissions, applicable approval requirements, and closed-period controls govern changes. OAuth tool calls are recorded in your connection audit history. Reading attachment content includes an AI safety scan that counts toward your company Ember AI spend.
