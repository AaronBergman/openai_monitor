## How it works

Connect your Mercury account to ChatGPT to get answers about your finances in the same place you’re already doing the rest of your work.

### Compare business spending

Review spending by category across quarters to see how business costs are changing.

### Track monthly cash burn

Chart cash burn over time and calculate monthly changes to inform financial planning.

### Check account balances and transfers

Look up account balances and recent outgoing transfers to understand where business funds have moved.

## Sample prompts

Compare software spending

Show what we spent on software last quarter and compare it with each of the prior four quarters.

Review six months of cash burn

Graph our cash burn for the past six months and show the month-over-month percentage change.

Review payroll balance and transfers

Show the current balance in our Payroll account and list all outgoing transfers from that account in the past 30 days.
