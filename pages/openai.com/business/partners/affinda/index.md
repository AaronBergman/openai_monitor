# Affinda (formerly Pathfindr)

[Visit website(opens in a new window)](<https://www.affinda.com/>)

## About

Affinda’s products help you control AI costs without cutting quality, automate workflows, and drive competitive advantage. Affinda's Deployment offering (formerly Pathfindr) also partners with you to build the skills and mindset your people need to use AI well, and deploy AI for complex, real-world use cases in your organisation.
