[Visit website(opens in a new window)](<https://www.sb-oai.co.jp/>)

## About

SB OAI Japan is a joint venture established by SoftBank Group and OpenAI to accelerate the use of advanced AI among Japanese enterprises. By combining OpenAI's advanced AI technologies with SoftBank's implementation capabilities, enterprise customer base, and operational expertise developed in the Japanese market, SB OAI Japan enables AI adoption tailored to each company's business challenges and existing systems. From building AI agent use cases to improving company-wide productivity and maximizing management efficiency, the company provides end-to-end support that helps drive AI-enabled enterprise transformation. In the cybersecurity domain, SB OAI Japan also applies frontier AI to vulnerability assessment and countermeasure planning, enhancing the safety and business continuity of enterprise systems in the AI era. Through these initiatives, SB OAI Japan steadily advances both AI adoption and security among Japanese enterprises.
