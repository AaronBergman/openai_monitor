# SpecterOps

[Visit website(opens in a new window)](<https://specterops.io/platform/adversary-operations/>)

## About

From AI-Enhanced Red Team Assessments to AI Identity Attack Path Management, SpecterOps’ expert advisors work alongside your team to integrate OpenAI GPT Cyber Models into authorized assessment workflows, from an initial engagement to a repeatable practice. Let us help you simulate, deploy, or build a sustainable program for AI-enhanced security. 

A Daybreak Activation Engagement with OpenAI and SpecterOps often includes: 

* AI-Enhanced Red Team Assessments - for teams that want an expert-led offensive security assessment of a defined environment   
* Operational Infrastructure for AI Red Teaming - for teams needing isolated infrastructure for authorized red team operations   
* Advisory Services - for teams determining where AI-assisted security fits in their program
