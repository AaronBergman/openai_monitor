[Visit website(opens in a new window)](<https://www.samsungsds.com/en/>)

## About

Samsung SDS helps enterprises turn AI ambition into measurable business impact. Our integrated AI stack combines enterprise-grade infrastructure, platforms, and AI solutions with end-to-end services spanning assessment, consulting, implementation, operations, and continuous optimization.

Backed by more than 40 years of IT expertise and deep industry knowledge, we design secure, scalable AI environments tailored to each customer's data, existing systems, and business priorities. In addition, Samsung SDS provides global digital logistics services through Cello Square.

As a trusted partner for enterprise AI transformation, Samsung SDS helps organizations accelerate AI adoption, scale innovation, and create lasting business value.
