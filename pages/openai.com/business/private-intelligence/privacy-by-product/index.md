## Control AI access, action, and use for every knowledge worker

With ChatGPT Work for enterprises, choose the app connections, control supported actions, and set automation permissions so teams can work within your organization’s boundaries.

![ChatGPT explains that an organization’s browser policy blocks uploading a Google Slides presentation to Dropbox.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/3JX15xqSZC17HSpyQOdVOT/63f0e6be6608ab5c376b75605f81c2f2/privacy-by-product-chatgpt-work-horizontal.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Role-based access controls

Manage access as employees join, change roles, or leave. Use single sign-on for workspace access or leverage Enterprise for automated provisioning and role-based controls for the capabilities available to each team.

[Explore workspace access controls⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/work-admin-faq>)

#### Sharing and publishing controls

Let teams collaborate on pages, files, presentations, and Sites while you manage who can share or publish. Use each asset’s sharing controls to choose its audience and collaborators. Connected company data retains its own access controls.

[Explore sharing controls⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/chatgpt-space>)

[Explore Sites permissions⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/sites>)

#### Strict limits on OpenAI access

Strict policies limit who at OpenAI can access your business conversations and for what purpose.

[See access commitments by product⁠](<https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/>)
