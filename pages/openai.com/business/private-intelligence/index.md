## Privacy should advance as fast as intelligence

Enterprises need control over how AI works with their information. We built the protections that let them bring advanced intelligence to their most sensitive work.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2kc3SI7hTcCrMi9tHrGZDd/ee801b2268e1fab04599eddc835322a0/privacy-by-product-work-human-access-role-based-access.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Access restrictions

We set strict limits on our access to business content, and provide ZDR and Private Inference for eligible workflows.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/3W3eHLh06vgYoec1SApPZY/0c2d871800226ed9938cce5f96bee7c6/privacy-by-product-work-data-ownership-choose-which-company-apps.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Data ownership

You own your business data, including your inputs and outputs.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6BGkgRLwIlqG55daRKEVOc/800900293132fb0f97548ae108c77f67/privacy-by-product-work-agent-controls-auto-review-approvals.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Agent actions

You decide what agents can access, which actions they can take, and when they need your approval.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2JufUSxatYGV9KzgBSOOIW/944d36a21e0d8fed7031c443e2839ef2/privacy-by-product-work-data-security-customer-managed-encryption.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

#### Data security

We protect your data with encryption, strict access controls, and ongoing threat detection.

#### Zero Data Retention

Zero Data Retention, available for eligible API workflows, means we don’t retain your prompts or model responses after processing.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/3a3rjNv7nwJ3u7ZuA0JDg8/c2e43c8122ebab31f1b229376dfd4f2b/private-intelligence-private-inference-icon.svg?w=3840&q=90)

#### Private Inference (preview)

Private Inference combines confidential computing with strict controls designed to protect your prompts and responses while the model processes them.

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2tUcx5sfbww55HFJL9NL9O/1d18026b5fa76fedfd647e56b75d85db/private-intelligence-compliance-assurance-icon.svg?w=3840&q=90)

#### Compliance and assurance

SOC 2 Type 2 audits, ISO certifications, HIPAA support through Business Associate Agreements for eligible services, and FedRAMP support for designated government offerings.
