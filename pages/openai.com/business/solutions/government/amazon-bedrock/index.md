# OpenAI models on Amazon Bedrock

Add AI to the applications your agency runs on AWS. Build tools that compare reports, answer questions about agency guidance, and draft briefings for staff to review.

[Contact us](</form/openai-for-government/>)[Explore solutions](</solutions/industries/government/>)

![White government building icon on a blue, teal, and lavender gradient.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/16yYjWGOkQbod21ffdpUGr/f4b51226bcdd80690a52cb778110e349/amazon-bedrock-government-hero.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

## From public reports to a draft briefing

Add a briefing feature to your agency’s research tool. An analyst selects a set of public reports, and an OpenAI model summarizes the findings and flags where the sources disagree. The analyst checks the draft against the reports before sharing it with leadership.

## Plan your first pilot

Build AI into the tools your agency already uses, from comparing public reports to drafting briefings that staff can check.

[Talk to our government team](</form/openai-for-government/>)

#### Model and region availability

Start with the AWS environment your agency is approved to use. Check which OpenAI models are available in your Region.

[Check model availability(opens in a new window)](<https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/models-region-compatibility.html#model-regions-openai>)

#### Build with the OpenAI SDK

Follow a working example from connecting the SDK to Amazon Bedrock through sending your first request.

[Read developer guide(opens in a new window)](<https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/amazon-bedrock>)

#### Amazon Bedrock in AWS GovCloud (US)

If your agency uses AWS GovCloud (US), check which Bedrock features are available and how they differ from commercial Regions.

[Explore Bedrock in GovCloud(opens in a new window)](<https://docs.aws.amazon.com/govcloud-us/latest/UserGuide/govcloud-bedrock.html>)

#### Buy through OneGov

Eligible U.S. government organizations can get ChatGPT for their staff through GSA’s OneGov program.

[Explore OneGov](</index/expanding-ai-access-us-government/>)

## What would you build first?

Bring a workflow your agency wants to improve. We’ll talk through what you could build on Amazon Bedrock and what it would take to try it.
