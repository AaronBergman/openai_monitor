# Our approach to public policy

Our mission is to ensure that AGI benefits all of humanity. Learn how we approach AI policy, governance, and the opportunities and challenges created by AI.

## Our principles

AI has the potential to reshape how people work, learn, and create. These principles guide how we build AI and engage in public policy.

Democratization

Resisting the potential for AI to consolidate power in the hands of the few.

Empowerment

Helping people achieve their goals, learn more, and pursue what matters most to them.

Prosperity

Building a future where everyone can have an excellent life.

Resilience

Working with governments, industry, and society to address the risks introduced by AI.

Adaptability

Updating our approaches as we learn more about an unpredictable future.
