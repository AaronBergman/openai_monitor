# Introducing OpenWorks

We’re building a nationwide network of OpenWorks Partners to provide free, hands-on AI support to small businesses.

[Become an OpenWorks Partner](</form/openworks-partner/>)[Get support for your business](</form/openworks-business/>)

## Help us reach every community in America

Small businesses power the American economy and shape daily life in our communities. With OpenWorks, you can put your AI skills to work for the businesses where you live.

We fund and equip OpenWorks Partners to work alongside small business owners on projects they choose. You might help a business reach new customers, launch an ad campaign, or build a tool their team can keep using on its own.

We’re recruiting our first OpenWorks Partners. Sign up to bring OpenWorks to your hometown.

### Become an OpenWorks Partner

Put your AI expertise to work where you live. Help local business owners discover what AI can do for their businesses and build the skills to use it independently.

**What you’ll contribute**

  * Dedicate days each month to work directly with local businesses

  * Host a monthly workshop in your community

  * Help owners and employees build practical AI skills




**What you’ll get**

  * A growing community of fellow OpenWorks Partners

  * Tools, resources, and workshop support

  * A monthly stipend of up to $8,000




**Who can apply**

We’re looking for technically skilled and curious individuals and small teams committed to helping local businesses thrive. Software and AI consultancies are encouraged to apply alongside their existing client work.

[Apply to become an OpenWorks Partner](</form/openworks-partner/>)

### Work with a local OpenWorks Partner

You know your business best. Choose something you’d like to accomplish, and an OpenWorks partner will work alongside you. Participation is free, and no technical experience is needed.

**What you’ll do together**

  * Identify a task or opportunity that matters to your business

  * Explore how AI can help and put it to work together

  * Build skills you and your team can use independently




**What you’ll get**

  * One-on-one support from an OpenWorks partner

  * Hands-on practice with AI using the tools you already work with

  * 3 months of ChatGPT Business at no cost




**Who can apply**

The pilot is open to independently owned U.S. small businesses, including restaurants, retailers, service firms, and manufacturers. Software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses, and private equity-owned businesses are not eligible.

[Apply as a small business](</form/openworks-business/>)

## OpenWorks in action

OpenWorks Partners are already on the ground helping small businesses in their communities. Meet a few of the business owners they’ve worked with.

Tiny Things Fargo North Dakota

![A shopkeeper looks up beside plants at Tiny Things.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/7aa6tcgDHkwnetFrFVoKCS/cb5490ae628061277ce0c2453951fd1c/tiny-things-story-photo.jpg?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

### A custom tool for a thousand tiny things

At Tiny Things, a gift shop that sells miniatures in Fargo, North Dakota, owners Jackie and Jordan Maahs needed a better way to track more than a thousand products and keep their bestsellers in stock. OpenWorks Partner Jake Ottiger showed them how to use ChatGPT to build a custom dashboard that connects to their existing inventory tools—a build that Jordan estimates would cost around $50,000 to commission. With clearer inventory information and hours saved, they can keep customer favorites on the shelves and enjoy weekends and vacations with their two boys without constantly worrying about the shop.

Ghost Grow 843 Charleston South Carolina

![A grower tends leafy greens in vertical growing towers at Ghost Grow 843.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/0lQhZN3xGHfUbauI8yfWb/de3e01457ff2291238faf784c728b7bd/ghost-grow-story.jpg?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

### A fresh approach to reaching new customers

Garret Fleming was knocking on restaurant doors to find customers for his microgreens and hydroponic lettuce. OpenWorks Partner Jim Reilly showed him how to use ChatGPT to find nearby restaurants serving microgreens, identify chefs to contact, and draft emails in his own voice. With more targeted outreach, Garret estimates his response rate rose from 20% to 75% and he gained 10 new restaurant customers within four months. He now also uses ChatGPT to source seeds and prepare grant applications, giving him more ways to grow the business himself.

Sign Badgers Fargo North Dakota

![A Sign Badgers team member sits at a desk in the workshop.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/17jLTP1EgCkUszUcfSMkve/958994f2e2ac1ba4b1f6914382769617/sign-badgers-story-photo.jpg?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

### Finding more time for creativity

Jen Robertson, the Senior Production Specialist at Sign Badgers in Fargo, North Dakota was spending hours every week tracking inventory and comparing vendor prices, which was taking away from the time she could spend actually designing and producing signs. OpenWorks Partner Jake Ottiger worked with Jen, showing her how to use Codex and ChatGPT to automatically track inventory and check for vendor pricing updates. Pricing checks that once took hours now take just 15–20 minutes, and Jen says the improvements save her two to five hours a week. With better visibility into the lowest vendor prices, the company saves money while Jen has more time for the creative, hands-on work she loves.

Black Paper Party Bentonville Arkansas

![Two women hold rolls of gift wrap in a boutique.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2twEo31Z8hD3X4Zuviyu0Q/5665081d5b9cc2c84e2474f09704680b/black-paper-party-story-photo.jpg?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

### Cutting costly errors for gift wrap founders

For co-founders Madia Willis and Jasmine Hudson, gift wrap and holiday decorations are a way to celebrate Black families and traditions. Behind the designs, they’re using AI to simplify the paperwork involved in supplying major retailers. They used ChatGPT to match purchase orders with shipping records. This helped them spot errors in vendor bills, dispute the charges, and avoid thousands of dollars in chargebacks. Now they can put those savings back into the business they’ve built together.
