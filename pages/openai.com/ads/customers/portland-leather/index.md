# Portland Leather reaches new website visitors with ChatGPT Ads

Portland Leather tested ChatGPT Ads to reach people researching their next bag and found that many who clicked through were visiting its website for the first time.

## Making discovery part of the next purchase

For [Portland Leather⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://www.portlandleathergoods.com/>), a first bag can become the beginning of a collection. The brand offers leather bags in a wide range of styles and colors, with customers returning to add another shape, size, or color to their wardrobe. Introducing more people to its products is an important part of that business.

The company already invested heavily in advertising across established channels. ChatGPT offered another opportunity: reaching people as they worked through the practical questions behind a purchase.

For a bag shopper, those questions might involve budget, everyday use, and how well a product will hold up. Portland Leather wanted to connect with people considering those needs.

> “We want to show up when people are asking, ‘What’s the best bag for my budget? If I’m spending $100, I want something that will last.’”

—Matt Fey, Director of Marketing at Portland Leather

## Connecting product interest with a useful next step

Portland Leather tested ads featuring its best-known bags, with different versions of copy, to learn which products and messages encouraged shoppers to click. The team explored interest across shoulder bags, crossbody styles, and slings, using the response to guide what to test next.

Each ad linked directly to the featured bag’s existing product page in Portland Leather’s Shopify store. There, shoppers could explore its colors, read about the bag, and decide whether to make a purchase.

![Portland Leather Goods ad featuring the Naomi bag and the headline “Naomi | Portland Leather Goods”.](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.openai.com%2Fmdx-assets%2F2b4549d5-6147-4ca3-9ea2-3f20a1d5d270%2Fportland-featured-ad.75818c0a.png&w=3840&q=90)

## Understanding who the channel was reaching

Portland Leather evaluated the test through Triple Whale, which its team had used for years to understand marketing performance and guide investment decisions. Using a shared reporting source helped media buyers assess ChatGPT Ads alongside the company’s other channels.

One finding stood out: according to Triple Whale, the team’s source of truth, 93% of visitors from ChatGPT Ads were new visitors.1

For a business focused on introducing more people to its products, that was an encouraging discovery signal. The team had expected some overlap with people already encountering Portland Leather through other advertising, making the reported share of new visitors particularly interesting.

## Building on new-visitor discovery

Purchases remained Portland Leather’s ultimate goal. Its campaigns had expanded from testing clicks and referral traffic into conversion campaigns, with the team focused on strengthening measurement and learning which products and messages could support further investment.

The new-visitor finding gave that work a clear purpose. For a brand whose customer relationships can grow from a single bag into a collection, reaching more people creates an opportunity to begin those relationships.

ChatGPT Ads gave Portland Leather a new place to pursue that opportunity: introducing its products as shoppers explored what they wanted from their next bag, and giving them a direct path to discover more.
