# WeightWatchers grows membership signups with ChatGPT Ads

WeightWatchers turned to ChatGPT Ads to explore a new path to membership growth. As the team refined its campaigns and evaluated performance through DV Rockerbox, early improvements in signup efficiency gave it confidence to keep investing in the channel.

## Opening another path to membership

[WeightWatchers⁠(opens in a new window)](<http://weightwatchers.com/>) is a weight management company whose behavioral program is designed to help members build healthy habits through nutrition guidance, digital tools, and community support.

Central to that program is Points, which translates nutritional information into a single value for each food. In the WeightWatchers app, members can track food choices against a personalized Points budget, giving them a way to put the program’s guidance into practice each day.

Growing membership means helping more people discover that offering. For the marketing team, one priority was introducing WeightWatchers to people new to the brand.

> “One of the things that we’re always trying to do is build some bigger pipelines that are more new to brand.”

—Jake Dmochowski, VP of Growth at WeightWatchers

WeightWatchers began using ChatGPT Ads to introduce its Points membership to new audiences in the US.

Ads directed visitors to information about the program and membership options, where they could learn more and choose whether to sign up.

## Connecting campaigns to the outcome that mattered

WeightWatchers focused on a clear business goal: membership signups at an efficient cost. The team moved from optimizing for clicks to optimizing for signups, using conversion data and Maximize results bidding to align campaign delivery with that goal.

![WeightWatchers ad with the headline “Track Meals By Points” and a phone showing meal tracking.](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.openai.com%2Fmdx-assets%2Ffd323ebb-63ae-48af-8601-dc1c1f22d5e6%2Fweightwatchers-featured-ad.7e5fe082.png&w=3840&q=90)

Across the full conversion-optimized period, WeightWatchers reported about 30% lower cost per membership signup than in the click-optimized baseline. Average daily signups increased while daily spend stayed roughly flat.2 For the team, it was a practical reason to keep investing: more membership signups from a similar daily budget.

## Building confidence through familiar measurement

WeightWatchers evaluated ChatGPT Ads through DV Rockerbox’s multi-touch attribution offering, which it already used to model conversions and guide media reporting. Bringing ChatGPT Ads spend into that system let the team assess performance alongside its wider acquisition mix, using a familiar measurement approach.

In a separate analysis, DV Rockerbox measured WeightWatchers’ attributed CPA on ChatGPT Ads at 15.3% below its blended paid-search benchmark.1 The result reinforced the team’s confidence in ChatGPT Ads as a channel for membership acquisition.

## Building on stronger signup performance

Together, lower signup costs and the DV Rockerbox results gave WeightWatchers confidence to keep investing in ChatGPT Ads. The team saw an opportunity to build on that performance by developing more creative and continuing to grow its presence on the channel.
