# The OpenAI Economic Research Exchange

A new research program advancing bold, independent inquiry into how AI is reshaping work, firms, institutions, and the global economy. Selected researchers pursue original privacy-preserving projects with support from OpenAI’s Economic Research team to help build the evidence needed to understand and navigate AI-driven economic change. See the inaugural cohort below.

To learn more about the Exchange, read the [blog](</index/introducing-the-openai-economic-research-exchange/>).
