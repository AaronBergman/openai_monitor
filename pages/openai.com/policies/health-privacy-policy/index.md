# Health Privacy Notice

We at OpenAI (together with our affiliates, “OpenAI”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respect your privacy and are strongly committed to keeping secure any information we obtain from you or about you. 

This notice explains our information and privacy practices when you use OpenAI’s “Health” plugin to connect Connected Health Accounts (as defined below) to ChatGPT (“**Health in ChatGPT** ”), and supplements the[ _OpenAI Privacy Policy⁠_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/>). 

Health in ChatGPT is only available to certain users at this time. To learn about data practices outside of Health in ChatGPT please see the[ _OpenAI Privacy Policy⁠_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/>). [_Learn more_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001036-health-in-chatgpt#overview-and-access>) about features, settings, and controls for Health in ChatGPT. 

Some of the information you provide to Health in ChatGPT may constitute “Consumer Health Data” as that term is defined under Washington state’s My Health My Data Act and Nevada’s Consumer Health Data Privacy law, and this notice provides certain information required under those laws. 

## 1\. Personal Data collected via Health in ChatGPT 

If you choose to, you can use Health in ChatGPT to link your ChatGPT account with third-party accounts that provide health and medical information to ChatGPT. These third parties include medical providers, Apple Health, and Android Health Connect (collectively, **“Connected Health Accounts”**). Connected Health Accounts allow ChatGPT to use connected medical records, lab and test results, prescription information, measurements of vital signs (such as heart rate and bodily functions) and other data related to your physical or mental health status.

We also collect other information you choose to provide about your physical or mental health status in conversations when using Health in ChatGPT, such as information about your and your family’s health history as well as your conditions, medications you take, symptoms you report, and health care services you have sought or seek to learn about. 

## 2\. How Health in ChatGPT Data is used 

We use the information you provide and that we receive through Connected Health Accounts to provide our Services to you, including to respond to questions you ask about that data, such as to help you learn about health topics and prepare for visits with medical providers, and provide you proactive updates, for example when new medical records are synced or when a new health trend is detected in your data. 

If you have [_memory_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt>) enabled in your ChatGPT account, conversations you have that use Connected Health Accounts can create memories to personalize future chats. If you’d like to have a conversation without creating memories, you can use[ _Temporary Chat_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/articles/8914046>) or turn memory off in Settings > Personalization.

We do not train our foundation models or target ads on data synced via Connected Health Accounts. To help make ChatGPT a more capable health assistant, conversations that reference Connected Health Accounts data, and content derived from it, may be used to improve our models depending on your ChatGPT settings. You can review or turn off model improvement at any time in Settings → Data Controls. [_Learn more_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7039943-how-openai-handles-data-in-consumer-services>) about how OpenAI uses and may review content. 

We also use the information in Health in ChatGPT:

  * To communicate with you, such as to provide you customer support;

  * To prevent fraud, illegal activity, or misuses of our services, and to protect the security of our systems and services; and

  * To comply with legal obligations and to protect the rights, privacy, safety, or property of our users, OpenAI, and third parties.




## 3\. Disclosures of Personal Data 

To assist us in meeting business operations needs and to perform certain services and functions, we may disclose information we collect to vendors and service providers (called “processors” under some privacy laws), such as providers of hosting services, customer service vendors, cloud services, and support and safety monitoring and improvement services. Pursuant to our instructions, these parties will access, process, or store Personal Data only in the course of performing their duties to us.

As described in our[ _Privacy Policy⁠_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/>), we may also disclose Personal Data: in connection with a business transaction; if required to do so to comply with a legal obligation, or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation; to protect and defend our rights or property; if we determine, in our sole discretion, that there is a violation of our terms, policies, or the law; to detect or prevent fraud or other illegal activity; to protect the safety, security, and integrity of our products, employees, users, or the public; to protect against legal liability; or with users and third parties you choose to interact or share information with.

We do not sell Personal Data of our users, including any Personal Data collected via Health in ChatGPT. 

## 4\. Choices and Controls 

We’ve designed Health in ChatGPT to keep you in control. Here’s more information about your choices: 

  * **Linked Connected Health Accounts**. You can connect with a variety of Connected Health Accounts, such as accounts with medical providers, Apple Health, and Android Health Connect. You control which accounts and data are integrated to Health in ChatGPT and you can remove or add new accounts at any time. See more[ _here⁠_. ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/articles/20001036-what-is-chatgpt-health>)

  * **Memories**. If you have [_memory_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt>) enabled in your ChatGPT account, conversations you have that use Health in ChatGPT can create memories to personalize future chats. If you’d like to have a conversation without creating memories, you can use[ _Temporary Chat_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/articles/8914046>) or turn memory off in Settings > Personalization.

  * **Deletion**. You can delete any conversations or memories at any time.

  * **Model Training**. [_Choose_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://help.openai.com/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance>) whether conversations and memories are used to improve and train the foundational models that power ChatGPT. 




## 5\. How to exercise your rights

OpenAI offers choices, including options to export or delete your Personal Data, through our[ _Privacy Portal⁠_ ⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://privacy.openai.com/policies>). When you make a request in our Privacy Portal, this will include Personal Data used in Health in ChatGPT. You can also contact us at _dsar@openai.com⁠_ ⁠.

## 6\. Updates to this notice 

We may update this notice from time to time. When we do, we will publish an updated version and effective date on this page. If we make material changes, we may provide you with additional notice such as sending you an email or notification.
