# ChatGPT Wallet terms

These ChatGPT Wallet Terms (“Wallet Terms”) apply when you use ChatGPT Wallet and you agree to be bound by them. Please read them carefully. They supplement the OpenAI [Terms of Use](</policies/row-terms-of-use/>) and [Service Terms](</policies/service-terms/>) (together, the “Agreement”). These Wallet Terms supersede any other or prior ChatGPT Wallet Terms that may appear on your device, computer websites or elsewhere.

## 1\. Wallet overview

ChatGPT Wallet lets eligible users save an eligible payment card and direct ChatGPT to use a payment credential linked to that card to facilitate the user’s purchase from a merchant.

ChatGPT Wallet does not create a bank account or stored balance, extend credit, transfer funds, or change your relationship with your card issuer. You must review and approve each purchase before it is submitted or grant ChatGPT the authority to approve each purchase in advance.

## 2\. Eligibility

To use ChatGPT Wallet, you must:

  * be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a contract;

  * reside in the United States, have a U.S. billing address for your credit card, and use ChatGPT Wallet in the United States;

  * have an eligible OpenAI account in good standing; 

  * comply with our [_Usage Policies_ ⁠](<https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/>); and

  * use a card that belongs to you or that you are legally authorized to use and that was issued by a financial institution in the United States. If it is a business card, your organization must permit you to store your business card with us for purchases.




We, your card issuer, the payment network, or our payment service providers may decide that a card is not eligible. ChatGPT Wallet may not be available on every device, account, merchant, or checkout.

## 3\. Adding a card

When you add a card, you authorize OpenAI and its service providers to collect, verify, tokenize, and securely store the information needed to make the card available in ChatGPT Wallet, for the purpose of making purchases with a merchant. Your issuer or our payment service provider may place a temporary verification authorization on the card; this is not a completed purchase and will be reversed after we verify your card.

Use only the secure card form. Do not paste your card number, security code or verification codes into a ChatGPT conversation. You are responsible for keeping your card and billing information accurate and current.

You may remove a card in ChatGPT Wallet settings. Removing a card does not cancel a purchase that has already been submitted or any obligation you have to a merchant or issuer.

## 4\. Approving a purchase

ChatGPT may help you find a product, prepare an order, or fill out a merchant’s checkout. Before the order is submitted, ChatGPT Wallet will show you the certain purchase details available to it, such as:

  * the merchant;

  * the items or services and quantity;

  * the total price or maximum price, including known taxes, fees, and shipping;

  * the card to be used;

  * whether the purchase creates a recurring charge, deposit, or authorization hold.




Review those details carefully. AI systems can make mistakes. Do not approve a purchase unless the details are correct and you want the merchant to receive the order.

What your approval means. When you select “Approve” (or use another confirmation method we show), you direct and authorize OpenAI to request or use a payment credential linked to the selected card and to submit the approved order and the information needed to complete it to the merchant and its service providers on your behalf. Your approval is limited to the merchant, items or services, amount, card, recipient, delivery details, and other terms shown to you. Submitting an approved order does not guarantee it will be accepted by the merchant. 

If a material detail changes before submission, ChatGPT Wallet will ask you to approve the updated purchase. We may also require additional verification for security or risk reasons. We may keep a record of the purchase details, the confirmation screen, and your approval.

## 5\. How payment is submitted

After you approve a purchase, OpenAI may ask a payment service provider, card network, or issuer to create or provide a tokenized, virtual, or other payment credential linked to your card. OpenAI may cause that credential and the order, contact, billing, shipping, and other information needed for checkout to be submitted to the merchant and its payment providers.

The payment credential used to make the purchase may be different from the number printed on your physical card, however, the purchase will be charged to your physical card account. Your issuer decides whether to authorize the transaction, and the merchant decides whether to accept and fulfill the order. OpenAI does not guarantee that a card, credential, merchant, or transaction will be accepted.

The merchant is the seller and merchant of record. Your purchase is a transaction between you and the merchant and is subject to the merchant’s terms, prices, privacy policy, shipping rules, return policy, warranties, and other conditions.

OpenAI is not a bank, card issuer, payment network, lender, or merchant for your ChatGPT Wallet purchases. OpenAI does not hold your purchase funds. Unless we clearly say otherwise for a particular service, OpenAI does not control and is not responsible for a merchant’s products or services, inventory, descriptions, pricing, delivery, safety, legality, refunds, or customer support.

A merchant’s appearance in ChatGPT does not mean OpenAI endorses the merchant or its products.

## 8\. Security and unauthorized use

Keep your OpenAI account, devices, passkeys, passwords, and authentication methods secure. Do not let another person use your ChatGPT Wallet or approve a purchase unless they are authorized to act for you.

We may require a passkey, device confirmation, biometric or passcode check, or another verification method before a card is added or a purchase is submitted. If a biometric check is performed locally by your device, OpenAI does not receive the underlying fingerprint or face scan.

Tell OpenAI promptly if you believe your account or ChatGPT Wallet has been compromised. Also contact your issuer if a card is lost or stolen or if you see an unauthorized charge. We may suspend ChatGPT Wallet or a credential while we investigate, and you agree to provide reasonably requested information about suspected fraud or misuse.

We may decline to facilitate, delay, or block a transaction, or suspend or end ChatGPT Wallet access, when reasonably necessary to protect users or third parties, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with law or network rules, address technical problems, or enforce the Agreement. When reasonable, we will tell you what happened.

## 10\. Removing cards; ending Wallet

You can remove a card or stop using ChatGPT Wallet at any time. OpenAI may end ChatGPT Wallet or remove a card or credential as described above. Ending ChatGPT Wallet does not cancel an order already accepted by a merchant, reverse an authorized charge, or end obligations under your agreement with a merchant or issuer.

We and our service providers may retain certain ChatGPT Wallet information after you remove a card or close ChatGPT Wallet when needed to complete a transaction, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, comply with law, or enforce the Agreement, as described in the ChatGPT Wallet Privacy Notice.
