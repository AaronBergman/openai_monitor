### Trusted by leading teams

See how organizations are using ChatGPT Work to move faster and unlock new ways of operating.

> “As part of Virgin Atlantic’s five-year digital strategy, ChatGPT Work helped us benchmark our customer journeys against competitors in a way we could never do before. We gave it structured journeys, had it research and walk through competitor experiences, then produce a dataset showing where we were outperforming, where we were lagging, and where we needed to invest. A competitor analysis cycle that would normally take weeks now takes hours, helping us move from insight to product decisions much faster.”

Nathan Bolt, Head of Digital Products, Virgin Atlantic

> “At Zapier, our No Lead Left Behind initiative started with thousands of strong inbound leads and a lot of manual triage. A single lead could take 35 to 45 minutes to inspect across HubSpot, Gong, email touchpoints, and other platforms. With ChatGPT Work, I built a QA/QC and analysis system that traced each lead’s journey, generated visual maps of every touchpoint, surfaced the drop-offs keeping people from booking meetings, and rolled the impact into a weekly exec dashboard. That helped us identify and hand off seven figures in pipeline every month to sales.”

Angela Ferrante, Head of Enterprise Marketing, Zapier

> “For NVIDIA’s GTC events, I used to spend a huge amount of time in Excel, pulling account lists, tracking registrations, and wrangling post-event feedback. With ChatGPT Work, I turned that into an automated workflow I can run event after event, then share with global teams so they can adapt it for their regions. Before GTC, about 40% of my time was reserved for manual number crunching and analysis. Now that process runs twice a week in ChatGPT, freeing me up to work with the field team on strategy and customer success.”

Will Daney, Go-to-Market Manager, NVIDIA

> “At Shopify, ChatGPT Work acts as my operating layer for AI enablement. Every day, it pulls context from Slack and active projects into a second brain, turns it into follow-ups, and helps me execute repeatable work with custom skills. I’m also using it to run a research program across 3,500 non-R&D employees: finding high and low adopters, coordinating interviews, analyzing transcripts at scale, and extracting patterns we can use to spread best practices and raise the floor for everyone.”

Chris Jones, Lead, Applied AI and Enablement, Shopify

> “ChatGPT Work helped me turn scattered PMO knowledge, customer signals, product issues, and execution risks into a shared brain for our stakeholders. Instead of manually assembling status across Salesforce, Jira, customer emails, bug reports, and project chats, I can walk into meetings with blockers, owners, and action items already surfaced. It helped me scale an early access program from tracking 6 pilot customers to about 80 customers, while keeping every team on the same execution view.”

Vaneet Seth, R&D Efficiency Manager, RingCentral

### Create share-ready work

ChatGPT can turn context from your tools and files into polished documents, presentations, and analyses that better follow your templates and preferred formats.

### Make your work interactive—and keep it current

Sites lets you turn ideas, plans, and data into interactive websites and web apps that are easy to share with your team. Create dashboards, project trackers, launch calendars, prototypes, reports, and more—and keep them up to date as information changes.

### Connect your tools and workflows

With more than 1,400 plugins available, ChatGPT can pull context from the tools and workflows you already use to help move projects forward.

### Keep projects moving on your schedule

Create one-time or recurring tasks, monitor updates, and check progress from your phone when you’re away from your desk.

### Iterate on work side-by-side in ChatGPT

The new built-in browser experience in the ChatGPT desktop app makes it easier to work across your tools, files, and accounts, with support for multiple tabs and richer agentic workflows.

### Review the approach before work begins

In Plan mode, ChatGPT gathers context, asks questions, and creates a step-by-step plan. You can suggest changes or approve the plan to start the work.
