## 

Reach shoppers in ChatGPT

### Show up in high-intent discovery moments

Your products appear in AI-powered results when shoppers are actively evaluating what to buy.

### Deliver more relevant results

ChatGPT helps match your products to people based on shopper preferences and past interactions.

## 

Why share your product feeds

Improve how your products appear in ChatGPT with data that’s complete, current, and under your control.

### Expand your catalog coverage

Make more of your catalog available to appear across ChatGPT results.

### Share richer product details

Provide detailed information like images, pricing, and reviews to better represent your catalog.

### Get better placement in results

Richer data helps your products surface with more accurate information when shoppers compare options.

### Keep information up-to-date

Sync pricing, availability, and updates with your systems so shoppers always see the latest details.

## 

Built on the Agentic Commerce Protocol

The Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) is the open infrastructure for AI-native commerce in ChatGPT.

Today, it supports product discovery and more accurate, personalized recommendations through shared product data. Over time, ACP will expand to support the full shopping journey with deeper integrations and APIs.

## 

Frequently asked questions

### Can users buy directly inside ChatGPT?

We’re moving away from a standalone Instant Checkout experience in ChatGPT and prioritizing better shopping discovery and merchant-owned checkout experiences. Users can discover and evaluate products in ChatGPT, but purchases are completed on merchant-owned websites or apps.

### Are there fees associated with product feeds?

There are no fees on purchases that start in ChatGPT. 

### Should I build an App?

Most merchants start with product feeds, which power how products appear in ChatGPT. Apps are optional and are best suited for larger merchants who want more control over the shopping and purchase experience.

### Do I need a product feed if ChatGPT already crawls my site?

No, but feeds give you greater control over how your products appear, helping ensure more accurate and up-to-date information.

### What feed formats and providers do you support?

We support common formats and integrations, including SFTP, APIs, and leading commerce platforms and feed providers.

### Where is shopping in ChatGPT available today? 

Shopping is currently live in the U.S. and will expand to additional regions over time.

### When will OpenAI be ready to integrate my feed?

We’re accelerating onboarding of partners, and aim to have a self-service merchant portal later this year.
