## Ryan and ChatGPT Pro are translating live sensor data into real-time wildfire alerts

### Ryan Honary + Laguna Beach Fire Department

Ryan Honary is developing an ultra-early wildfire detection network to help firefighters respond faster and get people to safety sooner. Fire departments like Laguna Beach are already putting his technology to work in their wildfire planning.

OpenAI is providing Ryan with free [ChatGPT Pro⁠(opens in a new window)](<https://chatgpt.com/plans/pro/>) to support his wildfire detection sensor network. He’s using ChatGPT Pro to build tools that translate live fire data into clear and actionable alerts for firefighters, including a walkie-talkie feature that lets them ask questions about potential wildfires and get answers back in seconds.

OpenAI is a proud partner of keeping communities safe.

## Dr. César de la Fuente and GPT‑6 Rosalind are speeding up the search for new antibiotics

### Dr. César de la Fuente + his research team

Dr. César de la Fuente and his team are searching for new antibiotics as bacteria become resistant to existing treatments. Their ambition is to turn promising molecules into medicines that could save lives.

OpenAI supports their research with free and early access to [GPT‑6 Rosalind⁠](<https://openai.com/rosalind/>), a model built for life sciences. It helps the team interpret biological data and find molecules worth a closer look, so promising leads can make their way to the lab sooner.

OpenAI is a proud partner of a future with fewer deadly infections.
