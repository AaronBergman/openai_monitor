## Big story: GPT‑5.6 launches, alongside ChatGPT Work and a marketplace rename to "Plugins"

Three closely-related launches landed together today:

### 1. GPT‑5.6 general availability
**[GPT‑5.6: Frontier intelligence that scales with your ambition](../../pages/openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/index.md)** — GPT‑5.6 ships for general availability, following the limited preview covered in an earlier run (`previewing-gpt-5-6-sol`). Three models: **Sol** (new flagship), **Terra** (balanced/everyday), **Luna** (most cost-efficient); the pricing page (below) also lists a fourth tier, **Sol Pro**. Headline claims: state-of-the-art on "Agents' Last Exam" (Sol scores 53.6, said to beat "Claude Fable 5" adaptive reasoning by 13.1 points, and to beat it even at medium reasoning while using ~1/4 the cost); near-parity with Fable 5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index while running 61% faster at roughly half the cost; new state-of-the-art on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (80, beating Fable 5 by 2.8 points with under half the tokens/time/cost). Introduces a new `ultra` setting that coordinates multiple agents in parallel (4 by default, up to 16 shown in benchmarks) for demanding tasks, and Programmatic Tool Calling in the Responses API (lets the model write lightweight programs to coordinate tool calls instead of round-tripping every response through the model). Includes customer quotes from Cursor, and others on coding/agentic benchmarks. (Note: the page's own competitive claims name Anthropic's current models — Claude Fable 5 and Opus 4.8 — directly; reported here as OpenAI's own marketing claims, not independently verified.)
