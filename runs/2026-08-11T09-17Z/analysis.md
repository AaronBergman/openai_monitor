## Significant updates and new pages — OpenAI Daybreak (cybersecurity) relaunch

Today's dominant story is a coordinated relaunch of **OpenAI Daybreak**, the company's cyber-defense product line, spanning 4 new pages, 1 new form, and a full rewrite of the existing `/daybreak/` hub and `/daybreak/partners/` page.

- **[`/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows/`](../../pages/openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows/index.md)** (new) — the headline announcement. OpenAI introduces **GPT‑5.6‑Cyber**, a specialized cybersecurity model available through a new **Daybreak Red** access tier (alongside a new **Daybreak Blue** general-purpose tier). GPT‑5.6‑Cyber is trained to reduce refusals on dual-use security tasks: OpenAI's internal "Advanced Cybersecurity Completion Rate" eval shows it completing 95.0% of advanced cyber requests vs. 1.5% for guardrailed GPT‑5.6 Sol and 57.3% for the prior GPT‑5.5‑Cyber. Used internally, GPT‑5.6‑Cyber found **two previously unknown vulnerabilities in V8** (Chrome's JS engine) that chain to escape the heap sandbox; Google fixed the first (**CVE-2026-15903**), the second remains under coordinated disclosure. Also disclosed: 5+ vulns in a popular mobile OS (incl. an untrusted-app-to-privilege-escalation chain), 3 critical vulns in a popular database, and 400+ kernel privilege-escalation vulns, all still being remediated with partners. Under the Preparedness Framework, GPT‑5.6‑Cyber is assessed at **High** cyber capability (below Critical) — explicitly reconfirms it "was not involved in exploiting Hugging Face" (the incident covered in this repo 2026-07-21). New safeguards: mandatory hardware security keys for individual Daybreak accounts from Sept 1, 2026; push toward Codex "auto-review" mode over full-access mode.
