### 1. OpenAI Advertising Platform Expanded: "Ad Tools" Replaces "Conversion" Branding

**The biggest change of the day.** Three brand-new policy pages were published (all effective/published June 12–13, 2026), and two older pages were removed:

**New pages:**
- `/policies/ad-tools-terms/` — "Ad Tools Terms" (published June 12, 2026)
- `/policies/ad-tools-dpa/` — "OpenAI Ad Tools Data Processing Addendum" (effective June 12, 2026)
- `/policies/ad-tools-subprocessors/` — "OpenAI Ad Tools Sub-Processor List" (updated June 12, 2026)

**Removed pages:**
- `/policies/conversion-dpa/` (effective May 14, 2026 — now replaced)
- `/policies/conversion-subprocessors/`

**What changed:** OpenAI's advertising offering has been reorganized and significantly expanded. The old "Conversion" product (which tracked advertising conversions) is now one component of a broader "Ad Tools" suite. The new Ad Tools Terms cover three distinct tools:

1. **Conversion Tools** — carried over from the old Conversion Terms, incorporated by reference
2. **Audience Data tools** — new; allows advertisers to upload "first-party data" to create custom audience segments for ad targeting; subject to strict rules (no data brokers, no third-party enrichment, no inferring sensitive info about individuals, no identifying/re-identifying OpenAI users)
3. **AI Creative Tools** — new; allows advertisers to use OpenAI's AI to generate, modify, optimize, and localize advertising creatives; advertiser is responsible for verifying all material claims in generated ads

The new Ad Tools DPA establishes that for most processing, OpenAI and advertisers act as **independent data controllers** (not joint controllers or processor/controller pairs), with a narrow carve-out for "Restricted Processing" where OpenAI acts as data processor. This is a sophisticated, GDPR-aware legal structure for a major advertising business.

**Why it matters for a layperson:** OpenAI is building out a substantial advertising business. They now have the legal infrastructure for audience targeting (like Facebook's Custom Audiences) and AI-assisted ad creative generation — both new capabilities not in the prior Conversion Terms.
