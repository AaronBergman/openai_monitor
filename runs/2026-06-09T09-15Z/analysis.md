### 1. OpenAI Files Confidential S-1 with the SEC — IPO Option Now Open

**URL:** https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/  
**Page:** [pages/openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/index.md](../../pages/openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/index.md)  
**lastmod:** `2026-06-08T21:19:52.726Z` (new page, added Jun 8, 2026)

OpenAI published a June 8, 2026 announcement that they have confidentially submitted a draft S-1 registration statement to the SEC — the standard precursor to an IPO. The announcement notes: *"We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it."* They explicitly state they have NOT decided on timing, and that it "may be a while because there are things we want to do that are likely easier as a private company." The filing keeps the IPO option open without committing to a timeline. The full text includes a Rule 135 disclaimer: *"This announcement is being made pursuant to Rule 135 under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and does not constitute an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy any securities."*

This is a landmark milestone. A confidential S-1 means OpenAI's financials, cap table, and business metrics will be disclosed to SEC reviewers, and eventually the public if they proceed. It does not mean an IPO is imminent.

### 2. "Built to Benefit Everyone: Our Plan" — Sam Altman and Jakub Pachocki Announce "Third Phase"

**URL:** https://openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone-our-plan/  
**Page:** [pages/openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone-our-plan/index.md](../../pages/openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone-our-plan/index.md)  
**lastmod:** `2026-06-08T21:20:34.676Z` (new page, added Jun 8, 2026)

A major vision document co-authored by Sam Altman and Jakub Pachocki (Head of Research), framing OpenAI as entering its "third phase." Key assertions:

- **Phase 1:** Research organization focused on AGI.
- **Phase 2:** Product company — deploying systems, learning from users.
- **Phase 3 (now):** Making AI "abundant, affordable, safe, useful" for every person and organization. Not just frontier capability but universal distribution.

**Three stated goals:**
1. **Build an automated AI researcher** — an AI that can accelerate and increasingly automate research itself. Stated internal belief: *"by March of 2028 we may have a significant fraction of our research being done by AI systems in tandem with our own researchers."*
2. **Accelerate the economy** — scientific progress, productivity, economic growth, "widely shared gains."
3. **Give everyone on Earth a personal AGI** — universal access to transformative AI.

The document discusses power distribution, arguing AI should broaden rather than concentrate power, and calls for international coordination including a global body with authority to slow frontier development when needed. It also says *"entirely automating everything is not the future we want."*

This essay appears timed alongside the S-1 announcement as a public positioning statement for investors and the general public.

### 3. OpenAI Economic Research Exchange Launched

**URL:** https://openai.com/index/economic-research-exchange/

OpenAI is launching a formal program to fund and support rigorous external academic research on the economic effects of AI. Selected researchers will partner with OpenAI Economic Research, using OpenAI tools and datasets under defined data-governance safeguards, to produce causal inference and empirical evidence on AI's impact on workers, firms, and the broader economy. Applications open now at https://openai.com/form/economic-research-exchange/, close **July 5, 2026**, with notifications by July 31, 2026.

This is a companion announcement to the vision document — framing OpenAI as proactively studying (and wanting external scrutiny of) AI's economic consequences. Likely part of IPO-era public positioning.
