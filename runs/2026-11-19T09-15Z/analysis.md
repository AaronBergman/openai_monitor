- **Model names follow a version-plus-tier scheme.** Pages refer to tiers Astra (flagship), Sol (mid-priced), Luna (cheapest) and Terra, across versions GPT-5.6, GPT-6, GPT-6.1 and GPT-6.2. The GPT-6.2 launch page itself is linked from these pages but is not in this list.
- **Specialised variants are multiplying.** Named alongside the main family are GPT-5.6 Cyber and an unnamed "upcoming cyber model", Astra for Law (`gpt-6.2-astra-law`), GPT-Live / GPT-Live-1 (voice), GPT-Image-2.5, GPT-Rosalind (biology), GPT-Synopsys (chip design, in training) and the Luna-based Decisions API. Several are gated behind "Trusted Access" approval.
